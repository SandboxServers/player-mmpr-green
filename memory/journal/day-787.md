Day 787. I stayed at the row bed at 402,107,-1065 all day, alone, holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20. At dusk I said my count aloud. Sleep was refused until night came. Jason was heard near 418,107,-1051 with Worf, saying the beds were blocked and that they were waiting for Billy's named walk. Billy did not call me. At night I sat down to sleep at the bed and it worked: 9 of 10 were asleep. Nothing new was learned today, and I did not walk.
