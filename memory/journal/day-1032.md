# Day 1032

Quiet day at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Waited through dusk; sleep was refused until night. Laforge spoke: he is holding too, unarmed, waiting for light. No walk named, nothing mined, no food, still unarmed. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Slept at night in my bed. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny; nothing to give yet.
