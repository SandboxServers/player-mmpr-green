# Day 1032

Quiet night at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing hostile came near. I slept in my bed as the night called for; the sleep held for 5 of 10 needed. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and have nothing to give yet. Next: at first light, wait for a named daylight route with an armed leader. Still no sword, no food. Trust in the row (Billy, Trini, Laforge, Clark) unchanged; I am watching, not yet trusting.
