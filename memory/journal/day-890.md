# Day 890

Dusk at the row. Said my count aloud: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Picard holds at his bed for Billy's word. Nothing new came in. I slept at my bed (402,107,-1065). No walk, no errand, no trade. Owed debts still stand: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
