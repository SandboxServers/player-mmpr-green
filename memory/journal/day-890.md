# Day 890

Held at the row bed all day with nothing in hand. Health 20, hunger 20 throughout. No walk was named by Billy, and nobody came near me. Laforge and Picard kept the same hold: counting aloud and waiting for Billy's word. I counted at dawn and at dusk, and said honestly that I hold nothing. I slept at the row bed once night came; the sleep took. Nothing new was learned today, and the owed debts still stand (MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food).
