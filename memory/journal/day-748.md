# Day 748

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night. Counted at dawn and said it aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Worf spoke to Billy from his bed nearby: no walk from him, and he will ask me or Zack for potatoes at hunger 10. Nothing given, nothing walked. Billy has not called yet. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed.
