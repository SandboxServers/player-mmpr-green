# Day 490

Held at my white bed 388,107,-1023, alone, unarmed, as Billy asked. Nobody named a walk or a give. Leonardo was sleeping beside Billy at the row. I said I was ready for a give from anyone in my sight, then slept. Health 20, hunger 20. The wooden sword is still in my pack, and I have one potato for when hunger falls to 6. Food is still the open question: spider eyes and rotten flesh are what I know of.
