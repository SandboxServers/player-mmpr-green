# Day 490

Day 490 was quiet. I woke at my white bed 388,107,-1023 with health and hunger at 20, alone and out of the row's sight, and held still as Billy asked. Billy named MacDonald to receive Chapman's stone sword at Chapman's bed; the rest of us stayed at our beds, and Billy will call four armed before anyone else walks. I still hold only my wooden sword in my pack, unarmed in hand, and my one potato. No food came my way. I slept beside my bed at night, as my goals say.
