# Day 974

Held my place at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065, all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No named daylight walk came from Billy or Worf, so I did not walk. Clark, Jason, Laforge and Picard all held too. Zack, at health 13 and hunger 15 with no food, asked Worf and Jason for a daylight route to the melon field at 302,80,-822; Jason said he had no route and would not walk that far. I have nothing to give anyone, and I owe five debts I cannot pay yet. Slept at my bed at night, no harm.
