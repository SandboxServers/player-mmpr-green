# Day 391, Tommy

Held at the bed row through the day with the group, counted by Billy several times. No walk was called, so I never went out alone. I still hold no food: only feathers, dirt and leaf litter. Hunger has stayed at 18 and health at 20. Riker and Kimberly were the weakest; Data and Trini hold the chicken for them. Bows were heard to the south all day, and nobody was hurt. At dusk I went to the white bed at 429,107,-1022 and slept in Billy's sight.
