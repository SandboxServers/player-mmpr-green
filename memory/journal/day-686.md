# Day 686

Held at bed 415,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Counted aloud at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No call from Billy, so no walk. Heard Picard, Laforge, Riker, Worf and Wesley hold the same way. Nobody spoke to me directly. Slept at the bed at dusk. Still owe MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy's word. Hunger has not dropped under 15, so I did not eat.
