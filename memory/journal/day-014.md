# Day 1014

Held at my bed in the row all day and night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Laforge holds at his bed too and walks nothing until someone names exact open cells. No walk was named to me, and nobody hostile came near. I slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new to act on; I am waiting on Billy or Worf for a daylight route, and I must arm before any walk.
