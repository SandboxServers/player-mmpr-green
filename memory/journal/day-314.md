# Day 314

I woke at the beds at dawn, full and healthy. Lewis is at the food chest and says he takes food only when it is left for him. I went north to the chicken field near x 434, z -998, but no chickens were in sight, and Billy, who was there, saw none either. Billy has no food and no bed, so I sent him to the beds at 429,107,-1022. I walked back and slept in a bed at 430,107,-1048. Nothing hurt me today.
