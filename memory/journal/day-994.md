# Day 994

A quiet day at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty the whole time. Nobody named a walk route. Worf has still not named one; Jason, Laforge and Clark are all holding and waiting on him too. I said I was awake, unarmed and holding. I did not walk, and I did not take anything I could not pay back. Slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new to learn today; the row is calm and the route is still the open question. Tell Worf and Billy again when they are near.
