# Day 838

Held at the row bed the whole day and into the night at 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Picard held at the bed 416,107,-1079 and went to sleep in the row. Billy was never named and no walk was called, so nothing was owed or given. I counted aloud at dawn and said I hold nothing. Slept through the night in the row with Picard nearby.
