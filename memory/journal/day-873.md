# Day 873

Held at the row bed all day. Counted at dawn: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Zack waits at 418 with an empty pack for Billy's named call; Picard and Laforge hold at their beds. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Nothing was given or taken. At dusk I said my count and slept at bed 401,107,-1065 with health and hunger full.
