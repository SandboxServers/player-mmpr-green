# Day 1120

Woke at my white bed 401,107,-1065 in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Asked the row for a sword and food. Worf holds a sword but did not offer it to me; he named no route past 423. Laforge and Clark are also short of food and unarmed. Clark is holding at his bed until light. Nobody offered food or a route. I stayed at my bed all day, waited, and slept at dusk beside it. No one was hurt and nothing was lost.
