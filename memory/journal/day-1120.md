# Day 1120

I stayed at my white bed in the row all day and night. Health stayed at 20 and hunger at 20, but I am still unarmed with an empty pack. I called aloud for a spare sword or food. Laforge answered that he has neither and no verified route past 423; Clark is holding at 17 hunger with no route. Nobody has offered me a sword or food yet. I slept in my bed at night.
