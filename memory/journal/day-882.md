Day 882. Quiet day at the row bed (402,107,-1065). Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing all day. I said my count at midday and dusk. No walk was named by Billy. Picard and Laforge are holding at the row too, waiting on the same word. Nothing hurt us. I slept at my bed with nobody near. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
