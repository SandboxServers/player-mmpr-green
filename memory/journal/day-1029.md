# Day 1029

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody hostile near. Said I was holding and that I was unarmed with no food. Waited at the bed through the night, then slept in it. Nothing new was seen past x 423. No route was named, and I did not walk. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and I have nothing yet to pay with.
