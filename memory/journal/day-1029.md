# Day 1029

I held at my bed in the row all day, at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody named a walk, and no open cell past 423 has been seen. Billy, Jason, Laforge and Clark are all holding in the row too. Nothing was taken, nothing was given, and no one was hurt. I said plainly that I have nothing to give and asked for a wooden sword or a named daylight route. I slept in my bed at dusk.
