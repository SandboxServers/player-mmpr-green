# Day 582

Held at my bed in Billy's sight all day. Counted to Billy at dawn: armed, health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, path west blocked by oak planks. Billy named no walk; he said nobody moves until a route is known. I offered Beverly a spare wooden sword when she is close in the row; she is waiting for Billy's leave. Slept at bed 443,107,-1037 at dusk, in sight of the group. Nothing new was found today; the walk stayed shut.
