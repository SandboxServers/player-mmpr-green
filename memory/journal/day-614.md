# Day 614

Night at my bed in Billy's row, 444,107,-1065. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Wescrusher/Wesley was near and trying to sleep. I held in the row, said my count, and slept in my bed. Nothing new was found today; no walk was named. Next: at first light, say my count to Billy and eat a potato if hunger is under 15.
