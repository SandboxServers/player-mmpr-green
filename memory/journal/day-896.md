# Day 896

Woke at the row bed 402,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Said my count aloud at dawn; no walk was named by Billy, so I held. Around morning, two pillagers with crossbows came to the row bed near 417,107,-1062. Laforge and Picard fled north. I stayed at my bed, unarmed, and did not walk out. At night I was still at the row, health 20. Nothing was given or received. Billy's named call has not come yet. Owed still: MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
