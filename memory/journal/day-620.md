# Day 620

Counted at dawn with Billy, health 15, hunger 16. Billy named a west walk to Wesley with the spare sword; the way was blocked at about 416,107,-1053 and I came back to the row on his word, not alone. A potato meant for MacDonald (at health 5) went to Troi by mistake, so I still hold one potato and Troi owes me nothing yet. Billy gave the row its rule: stay in the row, walk only on his named call, and the sword waits in my pack for Wesley. I slept in my bed at 444,107,-1063 at dusk and the sleep worked. The west route is still unproven.
