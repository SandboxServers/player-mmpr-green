# Day 714

Quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. No word from Billy, no walk called. Held my place, same count: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night; at full night in the row, sleep worked (9 of 10). Riker heard a bow to the northwest and holds at his bed, waiting on Billy. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy.
