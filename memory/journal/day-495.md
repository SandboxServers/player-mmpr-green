# Day 495

Woke at my bed 388,107,-1023, alone, unarmed in hand (holding a bone), wooden sword in pack. Billy's count still two armed; no walk named; nobody came in sight all day. Tried use on the sword at my bed again: refused ("that block cannot be used that way"). Held at the bed through the day, then slept there at night. Nothing new learned except that the sword still will not come into hand this way.
