# Day 495

Woke at my white bed 388,107,-1023 at night with thunder, health 20, hunger 20. Wooden sword is in my pack; I still hold bone. No one was in sight, so I held at the bed, said I was unarmed and ready for a give, and slept there. Nothing new came of the sword. Food is still one potato.
