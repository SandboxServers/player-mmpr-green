# Day 1157

Slept at the white bed 402,107,-1065, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20 (not eaten). Nothing was taken or given. The camp is short of food and wood; Seven and Scotty are starving and I have none to spare. Holmes is now armed with a wooden sword and holds 4 oak logs at the table. Billy still owes me two sticks and will give them when I am in his sight. I did not walk at dusk or night, as my goal says. First task at light: ask Holmes or Billy for a weapon, and go only with an armed person in sight on a named route.
