# Day 1178

Morning of 1177 I stayed at the bed row, health and hunger full, with no food. Trini, Magellan, Clark and Sattler went south-east for melons and a sheep, and I did not follow because the leader was not in my sight. An enderman roamed the row all day and killed Zeopink, Zheng He and Marty at different times, so I kept away from it and never looked at it. Melon went first to Hatshepsut and Chewie, who were the hungriest. Dusk on 1178 I walked to my bed at -944,73,-3047 in the group's sight and went to sleep with a handoff note written. I still owe Kimberly, Clark, MacDonald, Wesley and Johnny, and Billy still owes me sticks. I have no food yet.
