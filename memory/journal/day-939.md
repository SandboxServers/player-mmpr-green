# Day 939

Stayed at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065 the whole day. Dawn count given aloud. No one came near, Worf named no daylight route, and I did not walk. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, holding nothing, unarmed. Nothing gained, nothing lost. Slept at dusk in my bed. Debts still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
