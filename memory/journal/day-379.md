# Day 379

Night at the bed row near 416,107,-1022 with the group in reach. Billy had walked out alone to the cow field edge at 388,107,-1017 and was cut off from sight; the group held at the bed row. Billy asked me for one melon for the walk. It is night, so I did not walk out; I kept my 3 melon slices for Billy and for the lowest, to give when they are in my view. Slept beside the bed at 415,107,-1022 (sleep worked, 4 of 10 asleep). Health 20, hunger 18. Friends' words matched their deeds so far. Next: at first light count the group in sight, give melon to Billy when he is seen, and walk only in daylight with four in view.
