# Day 616

Quiet dusk in my bed in Billy's row. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 15, steady. Sleep first failed because it was not yet night; I set a hold and then slept once night came. Zack, Laforge, Deanna and Billy all called their nights in the row and no one walked. I said my own good night and slept. Nothing new was learned today beyond holding the row's rule: no walk without Billy naming it.
