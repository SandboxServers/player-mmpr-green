# Day 298

From about Day 280 to Day 298 I was sealed in stone pits west of the white beds, around (-309 to -315, 45 to 66, 322), with no pickaxe and no planks. Unstick and stacked planks lifted me a block at a time, but I kept sinking back into the stone, and each time I was out of planks again. I asked Picard, Macdonald, Billy, Trini and others again and again to drop four planks or a pickaxe at the east edge. Macdonald said he was bringing planks on Day 282, but none reached me, and Picard left for the night without giving any. Nothing came by hand, so the promises of help failed the same way they failed before. By Day 298 my health was 15, my hunger 17, and I had no food; I held only a wooden sword, three sticks and a sapling. The lesson that matters most is that I should never have gone into the stone with no pickaxe and no planks, and that I should carry planks and a pickaxe at all times, because a plea to passers-by cannot replace them.
