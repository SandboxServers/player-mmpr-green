# Day 1081

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065, health and hunger full, unarmed, no food. I said aloud twice that I was holding and asked for food or a sword. Nobody near offered any. Laforge and Clark are also holding at the row with no food and no walk. No named daylight route was given, so I did not leave. At dusk I went to bed at my bed in the row. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
