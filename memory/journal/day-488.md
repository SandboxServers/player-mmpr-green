# Day 488

Dusk. I held still at my white bed 388,107,-1023, alone, sword in pack, unarmed and not walking, as Billy asked. Worf reported the row sleeping in Billy's sight near 413,107,-1041. I said aloud where I was and sleep worked, but it was still dim when I woke, so I did not sleep through. Nothing new to learn today. Food is still the problem: one potato held for hunger 6 or below.
