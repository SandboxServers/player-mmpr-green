# Day 1095

Woke at my row bed, health and hunger full, pack empty, still unarmed. Nobody named a daylight route past 423 and no food reached me. Opened the chest by my bed; nothing reported in it. Worf (hunger 5) and William (17) are short of food, and I had none to give. The row holds still, waiting on Trini's potatoes and an armed reader naming open cells east. I held, said so aloud, and went to bed at dusk in my bed. Nothing was learned new beyond the empty chest.
