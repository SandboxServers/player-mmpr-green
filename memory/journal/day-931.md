# Day 931

Dusk on 930 I waited in my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, and counted aloud. Nobody came to me except Picard, who holds at his own bed and waits for light. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night. The night came while I waited, and I slept in my bed in the row without incident. Nothing new was named by Worf. I still hold nothing and have no weapon.
