# Day 1034

Held at my bed in the row all day, unarmed, hunger 20, pack empty. Nobody named an open route past 423, so no walk. Clark, Laforge, Jason and Billy were holding too. I said I was holding and that I have nothing to give. Slept in my bed at night; the day passed with no harm. Still owe MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon and Johnny food.
