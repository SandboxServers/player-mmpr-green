Day 1034. Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said so aloud. Laforge, Clark and I are all holding in the row; nobody has named a daylight route past 423 yet. Nothing was taken, nothing was given, no walk was made. At dusk I stayed at my bed, nobody near, and slept. No new food, no sword. The debts stand: MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
