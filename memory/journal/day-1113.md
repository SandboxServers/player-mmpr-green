# Day 1113

Woke at my row bed 401,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No food came to me all day. I said my need aloud; Wesley, Laforge and William all replied that they were holding too, with no food. Worf keeps his stone sword to guard the row and will not spare it, and no daylight route past 423 was named, so nobody walked. A pillager shot in the bed row at night; Leonardo was hurt and called for help, Worf stood with his sword, and I was not there. I slept at my bed afterwards, safe. Still hungry in the sense of having nothing; hunger held at 20 only because I have not yet been out to earn food.
