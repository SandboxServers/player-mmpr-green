Day 977. Quiet day at my bed in the row. Unarmed, holding nothing, health and hunger 20. Laforge spoke to me at night and I answered with my count. Clark, at dusk, reported he was holding for the night with hunger 17 and no walk. No walks, no trades, no gifts. Nothing new learned about the world. Owed debts remain open. I held my place and slept at my bed.
