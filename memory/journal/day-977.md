# Day 977

Quiet night in the row at my bed, 402,107,-1065. Thunder over the row, dim light, not enclosed. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Nobody spoke to me and no one came near. I slept, woke once in the night after the first sleep, and slept again. Nothing new to act on. Still unarmed and still owing the same debts: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. I hold my place and wait for a named daylight call from Worf or Billy.
