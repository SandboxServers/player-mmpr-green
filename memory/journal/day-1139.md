# Day 1139

Woke at my white bed in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Called aloud for a stick, food and a daylight route; nobody offered any. Clark and Laforge both report no food and no weapon. Everyone in the row is holding, and nobody has named a checked cell past 423. I held at my bed through the day and slept there at night. Nothing was gained or lost today, but the row is still safe, and the walk will wait until someone names a route.
