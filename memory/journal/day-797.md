# Day 797

Quiet day in Billy's row. Counted at dawn: holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody came and Billy did not call. At night I walked the few steps back to the bed at 401,107,-1065, stayed in the row's sight, and slept. Nothing new to learn today; the debts to MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny still wait on Billy's word. Trini and Jason remain unseen by me.
