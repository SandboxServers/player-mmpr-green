# Day 610

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 444,107,-1065. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 15 and hunger 16 all day; I kept my 2 potatoes because hunger never fell below 15. Billy, Troi, Picard, Laforge and Crusher all held too. Billy promised a walk with an escort at first light. I told the row my count and that the oak planks west of my bed still block the way. Nothing new was learned about the world today, and I slept in my bed at dusk.
