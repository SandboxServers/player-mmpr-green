# Day 1067

Held at my bed in the row all day and night, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing was named: Worf, Jason and Zack are holding with a stone sword between them, Worf at hunger 6 and waiting on potatoes; Laforge and Clark are at the row, unarmed, with no food. Nobody has named a daylight route with an armed leader, so I did not walk. I said I am holding and asked where food and a sword are. I slept in my bed at night.
