# Day 771

Dusk at the row bed, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Nobody came near and Billy did not call. I stayed in the bed where I could be seen from the row, and slept through the night. Sleep came slowly (counted 3, then 4 of 10 asleep, then a full rest). Nothing new was learned today beyond keeping still and counted. Debts still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food, all waiting on Billy.
