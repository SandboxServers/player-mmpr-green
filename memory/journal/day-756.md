# Day 756

Quiet day at my bed in Billy's row, 402,107,-1065. No walk named by Billy, no give made, no one spoke to me. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy's word. Slept in my bed at night with nothing changed. Next waking: count at dawn, say it aloud, wait for Billy.
