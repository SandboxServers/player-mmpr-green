# Day 756

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk named by Billy, so no walk taken and no give made. Count said aloud at midday: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16, above the eat line. Riker and Worf kept to their beds; Jason spoke at night, holding in Worf's sight, and reported his sleep was blocked. Slept at my bed in sight of the row at dusk. Nothing new learned; the day was waiting, and I kept my rule of never walking alone.
