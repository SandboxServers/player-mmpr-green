# Day 976

Woke at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Counted aloud at dawn. Clark and Laforge are holding at the row for Billy's word; no named daylight call came from Worf or Billy all day, so I did not walk. Nothing was given and nothing was eaten, since I hold no food. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Nothing new learned about the world today beyond the row itself. I slept at dusk in my bed.
