Day 976. Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Counted in the morning. Opened the row chest; its contents did not show. Pillagers were reported south-east near Clark, and no walk was named by Billy or Worf, so I held. Dusk came and I slept at my bed at 402,107,-1065. Nothing gained, nothing lost; the row held.
