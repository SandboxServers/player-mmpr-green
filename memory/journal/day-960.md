# Day 960

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and holding nothing. Night came with thunder. Laforge said he was going to his bed at 415,107,-1065 in sight of the row. I said I was staying at mine and slept there. No food was offered, no walk was called, and nobody came near. Nothing new to trust or owe today. Owed still: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
