# Day 471

Held at the row all day. Made a wooden sword from sticks and planks, but it will not come into my hand; every try failed, on a bed and on cobblestone. Gave my spare sword away and it went to Clark and Zack by mistake, so none is left for me. Pillagers kept blocking sleep nearby. Gave the potato to nobody yet; I hold it for whoever is hungry. Slept at night beside the white bed at 388,107,-1023, in the row's sight. Next: ask Billy or Zack how they got a sword into hand, and be counted at first light.
