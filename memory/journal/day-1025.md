# Day 1025

Held at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065, all day and into night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing hostile near. Laforge and Clark spoke from their beds, both holding for a named daylight route to the potatoes. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Nobody owed me anything, and I have nothing to give yet. I said I was holding, then slept in my bed at night. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
