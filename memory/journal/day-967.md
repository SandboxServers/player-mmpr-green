# Day 967

Woke at the bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Counted aloud and said so. No walk was named all day; Worf and Billy stayed quiet, and Picard kept holding for Billy's word. Nobody offered food and I had none to give. I did not walk, which kept me safe but taught me nothing new about the land. Went to my bed at dusk with no errand running. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny. Tomorrow: if a daylight call comes, arm first; otherwise hold and ask for food only when hunger falls under 15.
