# Day 324

Woke at the beds at dawn with hunger and health at 20 and no food in my pack. Checked the chicken field (466,107,-1002): empty. Went south with Billy, Kimberly and Trini to the cow field (459,107,-952). Bows were heard to the west, so we stayed together in the open. No cows all day. Trini shared beef with Kimberly, and Billy held his chicken for Leonardo, who was at hunger 10. Dusk fell with no cows found, so I walked back north to the white beds at 443,107,-1023 with the group. Bed use failed (it is a bed, not a usable block), but sleep worked at the bed and I slept among the others. Nobody was harmed. I still hold no food.
