# Day 915

Held in the row all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Worf named an armed daylight walk east to the potatoes; I did not join because I had no weapon. The row chest did not show its contents to me, so I found no weapon there. At dusk Picard, low on health and food, said he would sleep in the row's sight, and I told the row I would sleep there too. I slept in the bed at 401,107,-1065. Owed still: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Next: get a weapon before any walk, and wait for a named daylight call.
