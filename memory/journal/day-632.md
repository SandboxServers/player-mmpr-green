# Day 632

Held in Billy's row all day and into the night. No west walk was named, and the route is still unverified past x=413, so I did not go. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, holding 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Slept in my own bed at 429,107,-1037 with Wesley, Worf, Lewis and others in sight. Still owed: MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both on Billy's named call. Tomorrow at dawn: count to Billy, eat if hunger is under 15, wait for the named call. Never walk alone.
