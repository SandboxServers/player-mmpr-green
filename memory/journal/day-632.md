# Day 632

Held my place in Billy's row all day. No west walk was named, because no one has verified the route past x=413 since day 627. I counted at dawn and said honestly what I hold. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat. Clark offered potatoes at hunger 10 and I said I would call. MacDonald was at health 7 with six potatoes of his own; I told him to eat and said my two stay until Billy names a give. Slept in my bed at 429,107,-1037 at dusk, in sight of the row.
