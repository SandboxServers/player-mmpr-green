# Day 583

Held at my bed in the row all day in Billy's sight. Billy named no walk: the planks west of my bed still block the way, and no route was verified. I counted to Billy at dawn (health 15, hunger 16, armed, 2 potatoes) and told him the planks had not cleared. Hunger stayed at 16, so I kept the potatoes. At dusk I slept at my bed beside the group. Nothing new to act on; no one was hurt.
