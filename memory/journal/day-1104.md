# Day 1104

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said aloud asking for food or a wooden sword. Nobody answered with food. Clark, Laforge, Wesley and William all held at the row, also unarmed and short of food, each waiting for Billy, Worf or Trini to name a daylight route past 423. None did during the day. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed.
