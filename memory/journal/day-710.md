# Day 710

Held my place in Billy's row all day with no call from him. Counted aloud at dawn: 3 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker, at his bed, is out of food and still waiting for Billy's named walk; I refused to give potatoes until Billy speaks. Nobody was hurt. At dusk I went to my bed at 401,107,-1065 and slept there, in the row, with nobody else in sight. Nothing was taken.
