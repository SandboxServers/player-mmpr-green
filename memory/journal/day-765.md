# Day 765

Stayed at my bed in Billy's row all day. Counted at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Billy reported three pillagers north of him at 6 health and ran back to the beds; I held at my bed and did not walk north. Riker reported a pillager to the south and went to his bed for the night; he is coming to Worf. No walk was named by Billy, no give was made, and nothing owed was settled. Hunger is still above the eating line of 15. Dusk fell; I tried to sleep at my bed, and the day's digest was returned.

Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. All wait on Billy.

Curious next: where sheep graze; whether the west way clears.
