# Day 778

Quiet day at the row bed, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, holding nothing. Counted aloud at dawn and said I hold nothing. Nobody came to me in the row except Jason's speech: first he was at his bed in the row's sight, then he could not sleep at his bed and was trying the other bed at 415,107,-1050, still in Worf's sight. Billy did not call. I held the bed, did not walk, and slept in my own bed at night, in sight of the row. Trust in Jason unchanged: I watch what he does on a named walk.
