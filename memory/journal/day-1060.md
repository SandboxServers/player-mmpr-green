# Day 1060

Woke at my bed in the row, holding. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Nothing came near me. Said aloud that I was holding and needed food and a sword. Clark, Laforge and Worf all held in the row too, and no daylight route was named by anyone. A thunder morning passed quietly. At night I slept at my bed, which worked. Nothing new was learned about the open cell past 423, and nobody walked. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Nothing to give yet.
