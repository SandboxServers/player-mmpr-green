Day 1077. I stayed at my bed in the row the whole day. Hunger stayed at 20 and health at 20. No one answered my asks for a potato or a stone sword. Laforge and Clark also hold at their beds, and neither will walk without a named daylight route and an armed leader. Nothing new came in and no route was named. I tried to sleep at dusk and at night; sleep failed in daylight and worked partly at night. Lesson: waiting is safe here, but food and a sword are still missing.
