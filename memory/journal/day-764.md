# Day 764

Counted at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker and Worf both asked for food; I held, as Billy has not named a giving. Worf says he holds his sword and waits only on Billy's call. No walk was named all day, and I did not walk. Night came; the sleep attempt returned only the overheard lines, so I have not yet confirmed a full night's rest. Still owe MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
