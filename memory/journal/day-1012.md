# Day 1012

Held at my bed in the row all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody hostile came near. Said I am holding and that I will arm before any walk. No named daylight route came from Billy or Worf. Clark and Laforge are also holding at the row and speak plainly about their food and their beds. At night I slept in my bed. Nothing new to walk on; the owed debts still stand.
