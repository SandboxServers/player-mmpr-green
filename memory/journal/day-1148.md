# Day 1148

Dusk at the white bed 402,107,-1065 in the walled row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Billy reported he is holding my two sticks near 420,107,-1049 and offered to hand them over once I am in his sight. No route was walked; the row is enclosed in oak and pillagers shoot from the south. I said I will walk only in daylight with an armed person in sight. Slept at the bed. Food is still absent in the row.
