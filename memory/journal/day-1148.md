# Day 1148

Held at bed 402,107,-1065 all day, unarmed, pack empty, health and hunger full. Nothing came: the sticks Billy holds never arrived, and no armed person walked the row. Billy says the row is walled in oak with no open cell checked; Laforge and Clark (William) are holding too, waiting for a named daylight route. Nobody has food to give. I asked Billy by speech to bring my sticks to the bed and did not walk out. Slept at the bed at dusk. What I learned: the row is enclosed and waiting is the safe choice, but nothing moves unless someone names a route.
