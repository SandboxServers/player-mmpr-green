# Day 460

Held at the 401 bed all of day 459 and into 460, in sight of Trini and Clark. Billy never named a walk, no spider came into reach, and I found no food or weapon. Health and hunger stayed at 20. The row is steady but thin: Picard (health 5.9) and Meriwether (health 7) are low with no food, and Johnny holds the only rotten flesh. A wandering trader passed south of me and I had nothing to trade. Zack holds a wooden sword and is ready for a cow walk, and Jason asked Billy how the sword came into his hand, which I want to hear the answer to. I slept at dusk beside my bed at 401.
