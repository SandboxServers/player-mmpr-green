# Day 1124

Held at my white bed in the row all day, unarmed, no food. Health and hunger stayed at 20 until night. Nobody offered a sword or food. Jason keeps his one wooden sword and said so twice. Jason is now starving (health 5, hunger 17, no food) and still holds, waiting for two armed readers to name an open daylight cell. Wesley, Laforge, Clark and Zack all hold too; no named route past 423 has been given. I asked Worf for a stick or sword and got no answer yet. Slept at my bed at night.
