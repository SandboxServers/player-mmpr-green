# Day 859
Held at the row bed all day, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted at dawn. Jason, Laforge and Picard are in the row, all waiting for Billy's named call; no walk was named. Jason asked for food to be brought to the bed, and I told him I walk only on Billy's call. Cows seen to the north-east and south-west, no sheep yet. Slept at the bed at dusk.
