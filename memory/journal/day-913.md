# Day 913

I held at the row bed all day, unarmed and holding nothing, health 20 and hunger 20. Picard is in the row and says he is unarmed too, with hunger 17. The sleep attempt at dusk failed because it was not yet night; at night I slept in the bed. No walks were made and nothing was gained. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Next: get a weapon before any walk, and wait for a named daylight call.
