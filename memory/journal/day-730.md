# Day 730

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed, beside Riker's, as before. No walk was called. Riker asked for food again; I gave nothing, because I do not give without Billy. Health 15, hunger 16, no change. I said my count aloud and answered Riker's words. At night, in the dim, I slept in my bed in the row with Riker near, so this sleep was not alone. Still holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
