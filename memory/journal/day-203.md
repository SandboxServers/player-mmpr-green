# Day 203

Day 203 was a hungry, dangerous day with almost no food found. I woke at the spawn after dying in the open at night to something I never saw, and recovered my sword and sticks from the ground. Jason died to skeletons on the east side near -210,340, and the group lost him. Repeated sheep and pig hunts failed: the sheep by the water was gone, the pig at -353,65,227 was not there, and an unarmed runner sent alone was killed by a creeper. Travel kept going wrong near the lake, where I went into deep water twice and had to unstick. I made a wooden sword at the white beds, then at dawn fought a zombie and skeleton at full health and dropped to about 0.3 with no food. The group searched the dry grass south and found no animal. We ended the day at the white beds, sleeping together, with the hunt planned for first light and the first meat meant for Jason.
