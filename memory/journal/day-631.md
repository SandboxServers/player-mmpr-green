# Day 631

Held in Billy's row all day. At dawn I said my count (2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows) and Billy named no walk: nobody has verified the west way since day 627, so we stay put. I told Billy I had only seen the bows drawn southwest. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat yet. At dusk I went to my bed at 429,107,-1037 and slept beside Billy's group. Nothing new to act on; the day was waiting, and the row held.
