# Day 631

Held my place in Billy's row all day. Counted to Billy at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Billy named no west walk; nobody has walked the west way since day 627, so it is unverified and he will not send anyone. Hunger stayed above 15, so no meal was needed. Slept in my bed at 429,107,-1037 beside the row at night.
