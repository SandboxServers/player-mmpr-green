# Day 553

Unarmed all day and still no sword in my hand. I counted to Billy at dawn, midday and dusk, each time honest: health 20, hunger 20, no food, no walk from me. Billy named no walk and no sword for me, so I held at the beds in his sight and went to sleep at night 553 beside his row, with Kimberly in sight. Nothing hostile reached us. The main thing today was holding to the standing order, not wandering off alone, and keeping the count true. The sword line (Laforge, MacDonald, then me) has not moved, and my debts to Clark, Leonardo and Kimberly are still open.
