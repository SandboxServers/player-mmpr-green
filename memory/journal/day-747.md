# Day 747

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. Counted at dawn and said it aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Nobody walked, nothing given. Riker held at the white bed nearby, also waiting for Billy's call. No word from Billy yet. At dusk I wrote the handoff and slept at my bed; Riker said goodnight.
