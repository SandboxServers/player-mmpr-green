# Day 625, Tommy

Held in Billy's row all day. Dawn count at bed 443,107,-1065 with health 15, hunger 16, one potato, two wooden swords, two bone, two arrows. Traded lines with Zack, who offered a potato; I declined since hunger was 16, so it stays for MacDonald. Billy named a west walk with Picard to Wesley at 416,107,-1050; the path was blocked at 413,107,-1050, so I came back to the row. Billy stopped it at dusk and sent everyone to bed. I slept at the row beside bed 429,107,-1050. No death, no give. Hunger still above the eat line.
