# Day 621

Dawn count given at my bed. Zack beside me with a potato, Troi's potato still owed. Billy named a walk to Wesley with the spare sword; Picard was to escort. The west way was blocked again near 413,107,-1050, and Billy called us back to the row. Billy then said to hold the sword in my pack until he calls the walk again. Nothing was given away. Health 15, hunger 16 all day; I did not eat, as hunger has not yet fallen below 15. Dusk came and I am in my bed at 443,107,-1064 with Zack close. Days since sleep: 2. Still owed: Wesley (spare sword), Beverly (sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food), Troi (potato).
