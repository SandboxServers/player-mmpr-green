# Day 966

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Counted aloud and said so. No one came near all day, and no named call for a daylight walk came. I did not walk, since I have no weapon. Night fell and I slept at my bed. Nothing new was learned today; the day was spent holding my place.
