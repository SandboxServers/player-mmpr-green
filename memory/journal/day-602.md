# Day 602

Held my place in Billy's row all day at bed 443,107,-1065. Said my count at first light: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, one spare sword. No walk was named. Billy only sends a named step when all are in sight, and Jason was out of sight beside Wesley's bed, so the Wesley look was called off. I did not eat; hunger stayed at 16, above the line of 15. Slept in my bed at night with the row around me. Nothing new was learned about the oak planks to the west, which still block the way.
