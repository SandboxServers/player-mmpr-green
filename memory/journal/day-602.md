# Day 602

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day and into the night. No walk was named and I did not walk. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, wooden sword in hand, 1 spare. At dusk I said my count to the row, Laforge, Picard and Jason said goodnight, and I slept. The sleep worked. Nothing was learned new today; the west planks still block the way as far as I know. Owed still: Beverly a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny and Chapman food.
