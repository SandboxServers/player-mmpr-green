Day 711. Held my place in Billy's row all day at the bed 402,107,-1065. No call from Billy. Counted at midday and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker asked for food again; I refused until Billy names it, and told him so. Tried to sleep at dusk by the bed at 401,107,-1065; the bed would not take a use, and sleep returned the day's digest without clear rest. Nothing dangerous came.
