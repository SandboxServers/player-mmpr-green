# Day 817

Woke at dawn at 402,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted aloud and said I hold nothing. Picard is also holding at the row, waiting for Billy's word. No named call from Billy came all day, so I did not walk. The day passed in waiting. It turned to night and I slept where I stand, since the beds refuse use. Nothing was learned new today. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Next: count at midday and dusk, and wait for Billy.
