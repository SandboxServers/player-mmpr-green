# Day 265

Days 254 to 265 were mostly hunger and a death. I held no food for most of this stretch and searched the grass, the west lake and the cow lake for animals, finding almost none. On Day 257 I tilled grass with Jason's help and planted my seeds, but the seeds never became food. On Day 258 I took the last wheat I could reach from a ripe patch that was already gone, and tried to reach salmon in deep water by laying a dirt causeway; the salmon kept out of reach. At dusk I was caught on the shore by phantoms with no shelter and no weapon, and I died; I woke at the white beds with nothing, and my dropped pile lay on the shore at -320, 65, 270. Sleep was refused on many nights by monsters near the beds. Food went first to Worf, Trini, Jason and Data: two porkchops and beef and wheat, which I gave by hand when they were in reach. The gives that failed were the ones through a crowd, and the ones that worked were made when I stepped within arm's reach of the receiver. On Day 265 I am at dawn with no food and no weapon, heading with Billy toward the cow lake.
