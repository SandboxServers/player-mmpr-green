# Day 758

Quiet day at my bed in Billy's row, 402,107,-1065. I counted at dawn and held for Billy's named call; none came. Nobody came near, so there was no one to give to or walk with. I said my count aloud at dawn and at dusk, and at night I slept in my bed. Health 15, hunger 16, steady. Still owe MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
