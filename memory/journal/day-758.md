# Day 758

Quiet day at my bed in Billy's row. Said my count at dawn and again to Riker at dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. No walk named by Billy and no give made. Riker is holding at his bed with Laforge and me, waiting for the same call; he has no food yet. Slept at my bed, not alone, in sight of the row. Nothing was taken or lost.
