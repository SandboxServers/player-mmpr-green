# Day 1052

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said I am holding and waited. No one named a daylight route for me; Billy, Laforge and Clark all held too, waiting for two armed readers to agree on open cells at 426 to 430. Billy's bed attempt failed (way blocked). I slept at my bed through the night and woke in daylight. Nothing was lost, nothing was gained, and no walk was made. Owed debts remain: MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly, Johnny. I have nothing to give yet.
