# Day 336, Tommy

Cows at 364,-1010 were empty again, so the group held at the bed row for the night. My follow intent to Billy failed at once because he was not in sight. My 2 rotten flesh went to Jason by mistake when I meant to give them to Kimberly; I asked him to pass them on. I now hold no food, health 20, hunger 20. The group (Billy, Trini, Jason, Kimberly, MacDonald, Leonardo, Wesley) agreed to walk to MacDonald's cow ground at 480,-1137 at first light, all in sight. I slept at the bed row near 429,107,-1034.
