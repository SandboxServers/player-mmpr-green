# Day 1057

Night at the row. I stayed at my bed, 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing was found and nothing was walked. Clark, William, and Laforge all hold at their beds, and no daylight route has been named by Billy or Worf. I said that I am holding. Owed debts remain: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. I have nothing to give yet.
