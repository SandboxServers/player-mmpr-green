# Day 377

Woke at the bed row 402,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 18, holding 5 melon slices. Trini asked after Jason, Kimberly and Billy; I said I had not seen them and offered melon. Kimberly fell to health 6.3; Jason and Beverly brought her chicken and she was fed in sight of the group, with nobody digging the pit cell 394,107,-1025. Billy never called the cow walk. At dusk the group gathered at the bed row and I slept beside the bed at 401,107,-1037 with Beverly in reach. Billy was not seen all day.
