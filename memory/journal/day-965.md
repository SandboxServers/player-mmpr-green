Day 965. Counted at dawn at my bed in the row: health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No call came from Worf or Billy for a walk, so I held my place. Picard, beside the bed at 415,107,-1079, is also holding with hunger 17 and no food; I told the row I have nothing to give. Nothing was learned new today. I slept at my bed at dusk with no errand running.
