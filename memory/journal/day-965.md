# Day 965

A quiet day in the row. I woke at my bed at 402,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20, unarmed and holding nothing. I counted aloud at dawn and said honestly what I hold. Nobody came with a named call, so I did not walk. Nothing was given, nothing was taken, and no debt was settled. At dusk I went to sleep at my bed as the row requires. Lesson: waiting for a named daylight call is the right course while I have no weapon and no food.
