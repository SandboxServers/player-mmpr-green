# Day 1094

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing came to me overnight. In daylight Worf (at 418) reported hunger 5 with no food, waiting on Trini's potatoes. Laforge and Clark hold in the row with no food; William is hungry too. No one offered a sword or food to me, and I have none to give. I said aloud that I hold at my bed and will not walk past 423 without a named route and an armed leader in sight. Nothing was walked. At dusk I am at my bed, healthy, and I sleep here.
