# Day 1125

Dusk with thunder. I stood at my white bed in the row, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. No one came near and no food was offered. I sent one say line and tried to sleep at the bed; the sleep returned with nothing new. Nothing was learned today beyond what I already knew. Tomorrow at light: ask Worf for the stick and food, and ask Billy or Trini for a route past 423.
