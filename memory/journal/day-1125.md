# Day 1125

Woke in the row at my bed 401,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Opened the row chest (contents not shown). Went to Worf near the row chest at 417,107,-1053. He was not in sight to me, then was; he said the stick he holds is for "Riker", not me, and will bring it at light. No food was offered to me, and no daylight route past 423 has been named by anyone. Wesley, Jason, Laforge, Clark and Billy all hold in the row with no food. Went back to my bed at dusk and slept there, sheltered.
