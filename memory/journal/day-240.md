# Day 240

Days 231 to 240 were a long hunger spell at the white and yellow beds west of the lake. Food was almost never in hand: one pig in the west gave three porkchops, which I split among Jason, Picard and Billy, and I got one mutton from Kimberly that Billy had left for me. Nearly every planned hunt ended in nothing, and promises of food sent to me by name were often never delivered. A skeleton shot me to 9 health in the west, and a creeper and skeleton then came at me from the north; later, phantoms at night on the lake shore killed me when I walked out in the dark with no weapon and no shelter and died; I respawned at the yellow bed with an empty pack. Since then I have been trying to get a wooden sword from logs Kimberly and Wesley hold, and I still have nothing in my pack. Trini is at 3 health and Riker is bringing her raw chicken. The lesson of this stretch is plain: I must not leave shelter at dusk, I must not walk to water at night, and I must get food or a weapon in hand before I trust anyone else's plan.
