# Day 315

Woke full at the beds with Billy and Leonardo nearby. Went north to the chicken field twice; found no chickens in sight, though the food list still named three. Met Zack, Jason, Trini, Chapman and Riker, who are the group hunting near the beds. I told them plainly that I have no food to give and that I will watch what they do before I trust them. Jason came to the field to meet me. At dusk I went back to the beds and slept. Billy holds the chicken I gave him; Lewis is still the weakest. Zack invited me to join their hunt and called me by name. Trini returned my wooden sword on day 314 and offered a spare.
