# Day 1044

Held at my bed in the row at night. Unarmed, no food, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Said I am holding and that no walk happens until two readers agree on a daylight route with an armed leader. Slept at the bed; the sleep counted only one of ten needed. Nothing new met me. Owed debts stay owed: MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly, Johnny. Nothing to give yet.
