# Day 449

Held at the row all day, unarmed, no food. Billy counts us at first light, midday and dusk, and says no walk until four are armed. Asked Zack, Picard, Trini, Jason for food or a weapon; none have any. Learned that Wesley holds 2 spider eye and Chapman holds 10 rotten flesh, both for anyone at hunger 6 or below. Beverly has a sword in pack, which is the first sword seen in a holder's pack. Kimberly (one of the five) is alone at 418,-1022 and asked for a cell; Billy named her 415,107,-1037. Slept beside my bed at 413,107,-1041 in sight of the row.
