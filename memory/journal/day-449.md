# Day 449

Held at the row all day in Billy's sight, unarmed, hunger 20, health 20. Billy's wooden sword still would not come into hand; nobody in the group has yet got a sword into hand. No food found, none held. At dusk I took the bed at 413,107,-1041, beside Billy's, and slept in the row's sight. Kimberly, one of the five friends, was seen alone at 418,-1022 asking for a bed at first light; I will watch for her in the morning and not walk to her alone.
