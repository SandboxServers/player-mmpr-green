# Day 1101

Woke at the row bed at light, no food, no sword, hunger and health steady at 20. Nobody named a route or brought food. Riker walked toward 423 to look at a plank and reported that Trini and Worf could not see each other there, so the way past 423 is still not confirmed. I said aloud that I need food and a wooden sword and will pay what I owe. No one answered. I held at the bed through the day, as the rule says, and slept at dusk in my bed in the row. Nothing new was found.
