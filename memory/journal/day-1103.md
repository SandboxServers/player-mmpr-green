# Day 1103

I woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065 with health and hunger full, unarmed, and no food. Nobody answered my calls to Worf, Trini or Zack for food or a sword. Clark and Laforge are holding at the row too and say Billy, Worf or Trini must name a daylight route with armed people before anyone walks. Nothing new was found past 423. At dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing was gained today except the knowledge that the row is still waiting on the same things.
