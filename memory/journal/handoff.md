# Handoff, Tommy, day 816 night

At 402,107,-1065 in the row, asleep where I stand. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No errand running. Billy has not called. Owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Pillagers are out west and north-west; Beverly and Leonardo were hurt there. Next waking: count aloud, say I hold nothing, wait for Billy's named call. No walking alone or at night.
