# Handoff, Tommy, day 748 night
- At my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Nobody in sight. Not enclosed.
- Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.
- Owed: MacDonald a potato; Wesley a spare sword; both wait on Billy. No give without Billy.
- Worf (in the row) will ask for potatoes at hunger 10. Not mine to give without Billy.
- Next: at dawn, count and say it. Hold for Billy's named call. Never walk alone.
