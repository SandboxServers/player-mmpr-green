# Handoff, Tommy, day 739 night
- At 402,107,-1065 in Billy's row, beside my bed. No one near, not enclosed, health 15, hunger 16.
- Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.
- Owed: MacDonald a potato; Wesley a spare sword; both wait on Billy. No give without Billy.
- Sleep did not take yet (bed use refused; sleep returned no change). Try sleep again at the bed.
- Next: at dawn, count and say it. Hold for Billy's named call. Never walk or sleep alone.
