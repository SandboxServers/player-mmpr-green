# Handoff, Tommy, day 740 night
- Slept in the row at 402,107,-1065 beside my bed. Not enclosed; Worf is in sight at the bed at 415,107,-1051.
- Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.
- Owed: MacDonald a potato; Wesley a spare sword; both wait on Billy. No give without Billy.
- Next: at dawn, count and say it. Hold for Billy's named call. Never walk or sleep alone.
- Lesson: bed use is refused and sleep only works at night. Wait for dusk, then sleep.
