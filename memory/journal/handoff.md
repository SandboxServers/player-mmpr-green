# Handoff, Tommy, day 386 dusk

- Sheltered in the open at the bed row, 431,107,-1033, near Billy and Lewis. Health 20, hunger 18, empty-handed.
- Billy counted me at first light day 386 and holds for four in his sight. No cow walk called.
- Going to the bed at 429,107,-1022 (beside Billy's bed at -1023) and sleeping in the group's sight.
- At waking: walk to Billy's centre and be counted. Walk only when Billy calls it with four in view. Never walk alone.
