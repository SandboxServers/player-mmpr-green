# Handoff, Tommy, day 715 night

- In Billy's row at 402,107,-1065, near Riker and Worf. Not enclosed. No errand, no walk.
- Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.
- Owed: MacDonald a potato; Wesley a spare sword; all wait on Billy.
- Billy has not called a walk. Riker holds, short on food; I do not give without Billy.
- Next: at dawn, count and say it. Hold until Billy's named call. Eat if hunger falls under 15.
