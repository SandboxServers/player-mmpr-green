# Day 1146

Died three times on day 1145, shot by a pillager with a crossbow while unarmed, and respawned each time at bed 402,107,-1065 with an empty pack. On 1146 I stayed at that bed through the light, nobody armed came into sight, and I did not walk. Billy and Zack never gave the sticks I was owed. Laforge and William are also empty-handed. At dusk I slept at the bed. Nothing is owed to me; I still owe MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon and Johnny food.
