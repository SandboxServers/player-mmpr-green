# Day 1097

Quiet day at the row bed. I woke, said my need aloud twice (food, a spare sword) and nobody near answered me by name. Clark, at the row with William, is unarmed and short of food too, and is waiting for Billy, Worf or Trini to name a daylight walk. Nothing was given to me and I gave nothing. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. I slept in my bed at dusk; the sleep returned with Clark's speech saying the same thing. No walk, no errand, no death.
