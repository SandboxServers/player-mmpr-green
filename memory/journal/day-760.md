# Day 760

Held still at my bed in Billy's row all day, 402,107,-1065. Nothing was asked of me and no walk was named. I counted my pack at dawn and said it aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Riker spoke twice from his bed, also holding for Billy's named walk. Nobody else came into sight. At dusk I slept in my bed, as my goals say.
