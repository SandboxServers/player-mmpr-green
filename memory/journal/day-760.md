# Day 760

Held my place at my bed in Billy's row all day. Counted aloud at dawn and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker spoke twice, holding at his bed with no food and waiting for Billy's call; I answered that I hold the same. Billy did not come and no walk was named, so no walk and no give. Slept at my bed at night. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
