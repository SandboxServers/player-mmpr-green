# Day 1121

Quiet day at my white bed in the row, 401,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Still unarmed with an empty pack. I called aloud at morning for a sword or food. Clark and Laforge answered; both are empty-handed too and wait on a named daylight route past 423. Nobody offered anything, and no route was named. I slept at dusk at my bed. Nothing new was learned beyond who is waiting in the row.
