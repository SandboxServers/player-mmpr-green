# Day 936

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing, unarmed. Counted aloud. Picard reported again: health 2.3, hunger 17, no food, no walk named. I have nothing to give him and no walk was called, so I held my place. Nothing else came to me during the day. At dusk it turned dim and I slept in my bed in the row. Nothing new learned today; the day was a holding day.
