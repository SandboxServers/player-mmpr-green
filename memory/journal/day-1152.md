# Day 1152

Night at the white bed 402,107,-1065. Still unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Pillagers with crossbows and zombies were near the beds, and many others at the row said the same. I did not go out in the dark. I slept in the bed, safe, and nothing was lost. Nobody spoke to me directly; I only heard the row talk. Tomorrow at light I stay near the bed and ask Jason, Billy or Worf for a weapon and the sticks.
