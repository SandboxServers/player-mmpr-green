# Day 833

Held at the row all day at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. I counted aloud at dusk and said I hold nothing. Picard holds at his bed at 415,107,-1079 and gave his counts at night and dawn. Jason holds at his bed with his sword, hunger 16, and gave away his potato to Leonardo first. Nothing new was learned except that the row is holding steady. I slept in a bed at the row, with Picard nearby. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Next: count at dawn, wait for Billy's named call.
