# Day 1048 (waking 1047-1048)

Held at my bed in the row through a full night, dawn, a day and dusk. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, still unarmed and with an empty pack. Nothing was walked and nothing was named as a route. Wesley did not answer my ask for a spare sword. Clark and Laforge held beside me; Clark's health fell to 6 at one point. Beds took me at night. Nothing mattered beyond staying safe and not walking the dark.
