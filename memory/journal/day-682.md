# Day 682

Held my place at bed 415,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. Nobody called a walk. Counted aloud at dawn (day 681) and dusk. Jason, Worf, Riker, Wesley and Laforge all held in the row too; Wesley asked where cows or sheep graze away from the pillagers, and Leonardo has seen cows near 339,104,-979. A bow was heard to the northwest, and Riker stayed by the beds. Hunger stayed at 16, not yet under 15, so I did not eat. Tried to sleep at dusk but it was not night yet; slept at night and the night was skipped. Health 15, steady. Nothing new learned except where the cows are.
