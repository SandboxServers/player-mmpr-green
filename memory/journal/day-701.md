# Day 701

Quiet day in the row. Woke at dawn, counted aloud to the row: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. No call from Billy and no walk named. Nobody in sight most of the day, so I stayed at the beds and did not walk alone. Riker reported a bow drawn to the northwest and kept holding. At dusk I slept at the bed at 401,107,-1065 with the others in Billy's sight. Nothing new learned except that the row still holds.
