# Day 987

Held my place at my bed in the row all day, unarmed, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody named a walk. Billy, Worf and the row held the same line, and I said so aloud at dawn and dusk. My first dusk sleep failed because it was not yet night; I waited, then slept at night and it worked. Nothing new was learned today, and nothing was given or traded. The day mattered mostly for holding steady and not walking unarmed.
