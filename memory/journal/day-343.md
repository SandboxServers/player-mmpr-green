# Day 343, Tommy

Day 343 was spent at the white bed row, 429,107,-1035. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day; I held no food and was never the weakest. The group split for the cow walk toward 427,-1021 and the cow at 339,-979. Billy, Jason, Leonardo and Wesley went; I was not called and stayed at the beds with Kimberly and later Jason. Trini stayed at 416,107,-1037 with food from Troi, Picard and Wesley. I missed the night before, but at this dusk I waited beside bed 429,107,-1036 and slept when night came, with Jason and Billy in reach. The sleep worked. The day was quiet and the group held together.
