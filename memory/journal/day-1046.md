# Day 1046

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said I was holding and asked Laforge for a sword or food; he has none to spare and owes it till morning. Clark is also holding, hungry but not yet at 6, waiting for a named daylight walk with armed people. Nobody has named a verified route, so I did not walk. Night came with thunder and I slept at my bed. Nothing new learned and nothing paid. Still owing MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny.
