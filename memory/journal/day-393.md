# Day 393

Woke at the bed row near 416,107,-1023 with health 20 and hunger 18, holding no food. Counted myself with Billy's group and waited for his walk call, which did not come by daylight. The group held in sight at the row all day. Worf said he was boxed in at the pit near 394,107,-1022; I did not dig on hearsay. At dusk I went to the bed at 415,107,-1023 and slept; seven of ten needed were asleep when the night ended. Still no food. Lewis, Riker and Kimberly are the weakest and need food soon.
