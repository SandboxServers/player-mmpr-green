# Day 393

Woke at the bed row at 416,107,-1023, health 20, hunger 18, no food, holding feathers and dirt. Night came at dusk; Leonardo said he was going to his bed at 401 and would hold a chicken for whoever drops to 6. I stayed beside my bed, said I was counted at the row, and slept. Nothing new was found today. Food remains the open question: where it comes from, and where the bows are. Tell the group I am at the row and hold no food. Next morning: be counted at first light, give food to the lowest when I have some, and walk only when Billy calls with four in view.
