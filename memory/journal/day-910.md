# Day 910

Stayed in the row all day, unarmed and holding nothing, health and hunger both 20. Billy was out at the edge of the row with health 1 and no food; he would not walk in the dark and I could not carry him anything. Worf named a daylight walk to the potatoes at 463,107,-1073, but it was blocked and the group held at 418. Nobody has given me a sword yet. I slept at the row bed at dusk.
