# Day 689

Held in the row all day at bed 415,107,-1065 in Billy's sight. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Counted aloud at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Hunger stayed at 16, above my eat line of 15, so I did not eat. Riker, Laforge, Picard, Jason, Worf and Wesley all held the same way and said so aloud. Wesley reported that bed -1050 refused sleep at times and that Jason took it. Nothing new was learned about sheep or the west way. Slept at dusk in the row. Still owed MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
