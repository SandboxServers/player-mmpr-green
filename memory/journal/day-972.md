# Day 972

Held my place at the bed in the row all day. Counted aloud at morning, dusk. No named walk came; Billy and Worf did not call. Clark (William) is at the north bed, health 6, hunger 17, pack empty. Laforge is at the bed, health 20, unarmed. Nothing given, nothing owed paid, no food. Slept at my bed at dusk.
