# Day 1049

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1065 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said I am holding. Clark and Laforge are holding too; no named daylight route came. I walked nowhere. At dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing new was learned beyond the row's rule: no walk until two readers agree on a named daylight route with an armed leader. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny; nothing to give yet.
