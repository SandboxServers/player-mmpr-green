# Day 603

Dusk came with no walk named. I held my place by my bed in Billy's row, alone at the bed but with Jason near, beside his bed at 416,107,-1050, who told Billy he would hold and sleep there too. Hunger was 16 and health 15, so I did not eat. Ripe potatoes lie about 25 blocks east at 468,107,-1070. I slept in my bed. Nothing was learned new today except that Jason is in sight of the row and speaks plainly about his holding.
