# Day 603

Held the row all day in my bed at 443,107,-1065 with Billy's group. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Counted at dusk and said honestly what I hold: health 15, hunger 16, two potatoes, two wooden swords, two bone, two arrows. Deanna and the others went to bed in sight. I slept, and the day is over. Still owed: Beverly a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny and Chapman food. The oak planks west of my bed still block the way.
