# Day 603

Woke at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row, in sight. Health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, two wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk named; the oak planks west of my bed still block the way. I said my count to Billy and held. Jason, Lewis and John reported the same: no walk at dusk, holding in sight. Slept in my bed, no attack, nothing new in the digest. Hunger stayed above 15, so I did not eat yet.
