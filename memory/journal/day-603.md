# Day 603, Tommy

Woke in my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Said my count at first light: health 15, hunger 16, two potatoes, spare sword, the planks west of my bed still block the way. Billy named no walk, so we held all day in sight of the group. Heard Billy say he will call Tommy and Troi to him at hunger 10. Beverly is watching our hunger. Nothing hurt me. At dusk I slept in my bed, in sight, with no walk half-done.

Still owed: Beverly a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny and Chapman food. Next: at first light, count to Billy again; eat a potato if hunger falls under 15.
