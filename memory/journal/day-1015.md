# Day 1015

Held at my bed in the row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty, nothing hostile near. Billy and Jason were working out a daylight route to the potatoes at 463-467,107,-1076; no walk was named for me, so I did not move. Laforge, Clark and Billy all held in sight of the row. I said I was holding and that I have no food to give. Slept at my bed at night with no trouble. Nothing new learned except that the potato route is being planned by Billy and Jason.
