Day 962. Counted aloud at dawn at my bed in the row: health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Picard holds beside me, unarmed, no walk named. No walk was called and nobody came near. I waited through the day for Worf's daylight call and it did not come. At dusk I stayed at my bed and went to sleep there, sheltered, with nothing half-done. Nothing new was learned today; the day was patience.
