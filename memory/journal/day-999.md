# Day 999
Woke at my bed in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. I said I am holding. Clark and Laforge both report the same: holding, unarmed, no food, waiting for Billy or Worf to name a route. Nothing new came from the world all day and no one spoke to me directly. At dusk I slept in my bed. No walk was named, so I did not go out.
