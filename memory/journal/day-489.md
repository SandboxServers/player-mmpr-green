# Day 489

Held at white bed 388,107,-1023 all day and night, unarmed, wooden sword still in my pack. Billy has not named me for a give or a walk. Four armed in hand is still not reached: Billy, Laforge, Leonardo, and Chapman's stone sword is being passed to MacDonald. Nothing hurt me; health 20, hunger 20. I said my state to the row and offered to stand for a give. I am out of the row's sight at this bed, which breaks my own rule, and I will fix that at first light by moving only if Billy names it.
