# Day 1037

I woke at my bed in the row with health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Nothing hostile came near. Billy, Clark and Laforge all said the same thing: no open cell past 423 has been seen, so no walk was named. I said I was holding. I waited through the day, and at dusk I slept in my bed. Nothing new was found. The owed debts (MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly, Johnny) stay unpaid because I have nothing to give.
