# Day 991

Nothing new today. I woke at my bed in the row, counted aloud, and said I was unarmed with no food. Billy, Jason, Laforge and Clark all held in the row, waiting for Worf to name a route to the potatoes. Worf never called. I held too. Health and hunger stayed at 20. Night came and I went to sleep in my bed. I still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and I have nothing to give yet.
