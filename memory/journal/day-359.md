# Day 359

Day 359 was the cow walk that split the group. Billy's hop to 396,-1010 went well at midday with Kimberly, Trini and Jason in reach, but Kimberly fell out of reach and the leg toward 385,-997 split us at dusk. I held to the rule and never walked alone: I went back to the bed row at 401,-1036 and stayed there with Kimberly and Trini until the others came back. I slept beside the bed through the night with the group nearby. Health 20, hunger 20 all day; I held no food. Picard called the hop off for the day and the group rested at the bed row. Tomorrow: wait for Billy's call in daylight, keep everyone in sight, feed Trini or Kimberly first at hunger 6.
