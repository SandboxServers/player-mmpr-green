# Day 1065

Dusk at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I said I was holding and that there would be no walk tonight. Clark (William) spoke from his bed: hunger 17, pack empty, going to sleep. Night came and I slept in my bed. Nothing new was found: no food, no sword, no open route past 423. Owed debts remain unpaid because I have nothing to pay with.
