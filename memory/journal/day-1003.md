# Day 1003

Held at my bed in the row all day and into the night. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed with an empty pack. Clark was at his bed and told me to stay near Billy or Worf. No named daylight route came, so I did not walk. I said I was holding and wrote it down. I slept in my bed at night. Nothing hostile came near. The owed debts (MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food) stand, and I still have nothing to give.
