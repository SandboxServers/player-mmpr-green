# Day 871

Stayed at the row bed all day. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing the whole time. Said my count at dusk. Jason reported the east way blocked at 417, and Worf was walking to Billy at 430. No walk was named for me, so I held. Sleep was refused at dusk because it was not yet night; I slept after dark and the night was skipped. I did not walk alone and did not take anything from anyone.
