# Day 464

I held at the row all day at 388,107,-1023, unarmed and with no food. Billy called no walk: he has no spare wooden sword and keeps his stone sword for the walk. Trini is armed with a wooden sword, and Beverly has one in her pack. Johnny, Troi, Clark, Wesley and Data each said they had no weapon or food to spare, except Wesley's 2 spider eye (for hunger 6 or below) and Clark's 4 planks. Nobody named a walk, and the four-armed rule still stands. At dusk I slept by the white bed at 387,107,-1023 in the row's sight.
