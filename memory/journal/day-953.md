# Day 953 (night)

Held my place in the row all day at my bed, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No walk was named at dawn, midday or dusk, so I did not walk. Laforge, Zack and Picard were in the row; Picard was at health 2.3 with no food, and I had none to give. I counted aloud at dawn and slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new to learn today beyond the holding; the row still waits on Worf's named daylight call.
