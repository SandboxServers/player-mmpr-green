# Day 698

Held my place in Billy's row all day at bed 416,107,-1065. Counted at dawn and at dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16, not yet under 15, so no eating. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. Went to sleep at dusk in the row, in sight of the group. Nothing new was learned today; the west way and the sheep are still unknown.
