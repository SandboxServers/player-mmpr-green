# Day 835

Quiet day in the row at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, holding nothing. Said dusk and night counts aloud. Picard holds at the bed nearby and says he waits on Billy's word too. No walk named, no errand, nothing to trade. Went to the bed by the row at night and slept with Picard in the row. Nothing new learned except that the row is holding steady.
