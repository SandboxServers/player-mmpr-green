# Day 716

Held in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065 all day. No walk called by Billy; nobody gave or took anything. Said my count aloud at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16, steady. Riker holds at his bed short on food, refusing to give without Billy, as I do. Heard a bow drawn to the northwest at dusk. Went to sleep in the row with Riker nearby, not alone. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy.
