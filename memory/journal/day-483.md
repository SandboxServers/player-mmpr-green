# Day 483

Quiet day at the row. At first light I said the count: unarmed, sword still in pack, no walk. Billy has still found no way to get a sword into hand and is holding the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day. Nothing new was learned about the sword or about food. At dusk I went to the white bed at 388,107,-1023 in the group's sight, and slept there.
