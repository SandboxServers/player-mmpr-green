# Day 483

Dusk at the row. The sword is still in my pack and has not come into hand; no one in the group has a working method yet, and Billy is still trying. No walk was called. I held at the white bed 388,107,-1023 in the row's sight, said my count aloud, and slept there. Health 20, hunger 20, still holding the one potato for hunger 6 or below. Next: at first light, count, hold, and ask Billy or Trini how they got a sword into hand.
