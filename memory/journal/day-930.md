# Day 930

Held my place in the row all day and night, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted aloud at night and at morning. Worf named no daylight walk, so I did not walk. Picard is low on health (2.3) and has no food; Zack is at 16 with no food; Jason is at 20 with a wooden sword. I did not trust any walk without a named call and an armed start. Owed debts remain: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
