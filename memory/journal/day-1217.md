Day 1217. I held with Trini's group all day. In the morning I walked the east leg and the north-east leg in company, found only beds and signs, and no water or cows. A later leg took us to the Stores hall, where the signs say bread and cooked beef, but the chests hold no food. My hunger fell to 20 and stayed there; I took no food, since Trini's rule is to eat only under 15 after asking. Trini named no leg after noon and held the group at the first stop until night. I stood in her reach at -930,71,-3046 and slept there in sight of the group. Health stayed 20 all day. Lizzie, Chapman and Crusoe are set to search for cows and pigs in sight of the group. Jason and Brunel are still trying to give rotten flesh, and the group keeps stepping back to let them.
