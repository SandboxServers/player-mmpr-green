# Day 949

Quiet day at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, unarmed, holding nothing. No call from Worf for a daylight walk, so I did not leave. I said aloud that I was staying put through the night. Night passed without harm, and the day came again with no walk named. At dusk, with thunder, I slept at my bed. Only 2 of 10 needed were asleep at the last count. I still owe MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon and Johnny food, and I have nothing to give yet.
