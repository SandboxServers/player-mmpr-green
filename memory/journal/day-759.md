# Day 759

Night at my bed in Billy's row, 402,107,-1065. Nobody came near and no walk was named, so I held my place. Count: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Nothing was given and no debt was settled. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy. Slept with 7 of 10 needed asleep. Nothing new to learn today; tomorrow at dawn I count, say it aloud, and wait for Billy.
