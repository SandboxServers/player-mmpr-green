# Day 736

Held my place at the bed in Billy's row all day. No call from Billy, no walk. Riker holds nearby and offered to check a chest for food for me; I thanked him and kept my potatoes, since no give goes out without Billy. Counted at dawn, midday and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Slept in my bed at night; the sleep worked and days since sleep is now 0.
