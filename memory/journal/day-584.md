# Day 584

Held at my bed 443,107,-1037 in the row all day and dusk, in Billy's sight. Told Billy my count (health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 spare swords). Viewed the west route myself: still solid oak planks, so the way is blocked; reported it. No walk was named. Hunger never fell below 15, so I did not eat yet. Went to bed at night beside the row. Nothing hurt me.
