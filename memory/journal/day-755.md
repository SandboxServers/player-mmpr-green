# Day 755

Quiet day in Billy's row. Counted at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker and Worf held at their beds and spoke of holding for Billy's named walk; no walk was named and nobody came near. I said my count aloud and waited. At dusk I slept in the white bed beside me at 402,107,-1065, in sight of the row. Nothing was given, no debts were settled, and no errand ran. Hunger is still under 17 and I have not yet eaten today; I will eat when it falls under 15 or when Billy calls it.
