# Day 1119

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nobody offered food or a sword. Laforge asked about the planks at 423, which I have not seen. Clark is waiting on a named daylight route or food from Billy, Worf or Trini, and is at hunger 17. I told Laforge I had not seen 423 and asked for food or a spare sword. No route was named past 423, and I did not walk. Slept in my bed at night, sheltered, nothing lost.
