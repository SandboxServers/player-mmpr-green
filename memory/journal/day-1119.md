# Day 1119

Quiet night in the row. Walked to my white bed and slept beside it, health 20, hunger 20. No food, no sword offered, no route past 423 named. Nothing changed; the debts to MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny still stand. Tomorrow at light: ask Worf or Jason for a sword, and ask Billy, Trini or Zack for food or a route.
