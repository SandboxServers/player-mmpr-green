# Day 709

Held my place in Billy's row all day, with no call from him yet. Counted aloud at morning and dusk: 3 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker, at his bed in the row, asked for the potatoes; I refused until Billy names the walk, and said so plainly. Riker is short of food and holding for Billy's word too. At dusk I stepped onto the bed at 401,107,-1065 and slept in the row, in sight of the group. Nothing was taken and no one was hurt.
