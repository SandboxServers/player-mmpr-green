Day 773. Stayed at the row bed at 402,107,-1065 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted at dawn and again at dusk, alone, with nobody in sight. Billy did not call. No one came near and no harm came. I waited the full wait, then went to bed in the row bed. Three task-finished events came in during sleep; nothing was asked of me. The rule still holds: walk only on Billy's named call, never alone at night.
