# Day 1177

Stayed at the bed row all day with my wooden sword and no food; hunger held at 20 and health at 20. Fogg and Lara planned a cow hunt west, but Fogg could not see me, so my follow and goto both failed. Lara's group came back at dusk with no cow found. My debts (MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food) are still open, and Billy's sticks are still owed to me. Zack and Hatshepsut were the hungriest; Zack gave Hatshepsut a carrot. Lesson: when a leader cannot see me, I must step into their sight myself before the group forms.
