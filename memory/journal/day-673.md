# Day 673

Held my place in Billy's row all day. Counted aloud at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named for me, so I did not walk. MacDonald greeted me at night, and I answered with my count and the potato I owe him once Billy calls it. Lewis went to his bed for the night with no walk. Nothing dangerous came near. I went to bed at my bed 429,107,-1065 in sight of the row, and sleep started (4 of 10 asleep). Hunger is still 16, so I did not eat.
