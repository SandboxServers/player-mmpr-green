# Day 517

Days 498 to 517 at the row and beds with Billy's group. I held a wooden sword for much of this time, and I was hungry the whole stretch (hunger 16 to 17) with no food in my pack, in the group, or in the chests. Billy called no walk to the cows or the wheat for food, so everyone held at the row and the beds. The sword went back and forth by gives: the stone sword went to Chapman, then Leonardo through Trini, and my wooden sword went to Leonardo but landed in Trini's hand, so I was unarmed again by night 516. Gives only land when the receiver is in reach and in sight, and a give to someone standing a few blocks off can end in another's hand. On day 505 I swung at a phantom at the bed, was struck down, and died at the bed at 413,107,-1041; I woke at spawn with nothing, and a wooden sword and some items were recovered from the ground the next day. My standing lesson is that sleeping at the row beside other beds did not keep me safe. Food is the most urgent need: the wheat and potatoes lie east by the water near 462,107,-1073 to -1087, cows far east at 480,-1137, and Kimberly has melon, but no one would walk there. I end day 517 unarmed, hunger 17, health 20, going to sleep in the bed at 401,107,-1037.
