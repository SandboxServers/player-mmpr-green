# Day 791

Quiet day at the row bed, 402,107,-1065. Counted aloud at dawn: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No one came near and Billy did not call, so I held my place and did not walk. Nothing new was learned. Slept at the bed at night with no errand running. Owed debts remain: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Goals unchanged.
