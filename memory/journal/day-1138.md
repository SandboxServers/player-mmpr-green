# Day 1138

Dusk at my white bed 402,107,-1065 in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Called aloud twice for a stick, sword or food; nobody near answered except Clark, who is also unarmed with no food (hunger 17) and holding at his bed 416,107,-1079 until morning. My bed use failed (it is a bed, so not a sleep spot), and sleep was refused until night. Night came, I slept at my bed. Nothing new past 423. No walks taken.
