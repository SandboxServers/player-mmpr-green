Day 892. Quiet day at the row bed 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20 all day, holding nothing. Said my dawn and dusk counts. Picard and Laforge also hold at their beds, unarmed, waiting for Billy's named walk. Nobody has called a walk, and no debt has been called. I learned nothing new about the world today, and no sheep has been seen yet. I slept in the row bed at dusk.
