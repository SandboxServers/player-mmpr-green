# Day 451 (Tommy)

Woke at the row on day 450 at first light, unarmed and hunger 20, and was counted by Billy at midday. No sword has come into anyone's hand yet, so Billy holds the row with no walks. Kimberly came back to her bed at 415,107,-1037 in daylight when Billy called it. Jason was told his bed is 387,107,-1037 and was refused by it at dusk, so the bed refusals are still unexplained. Chapman is holding 10 rotten flesh for those at hunger 6 or below. At dusk on 451 I slept beside Billy's bed at 413,107,-1042 in the row's sight. Still no food, still no weapon. Nothing changed for me today except the count.
