# Day 419

I held at the bed row at 416,107,-1037 through day 418. Billy counted me at dawn, at midday and at dusk, with Kimberly, Picard, Beverly, Jason, Trini and Leonardo in reach. Trini was lost to phantoms and came back to the row. Leonardo gave his wooden sword to Billy's group, and it went to Kimberly by mistake before it reached Billy. Billy kept the walk to the cow field waiting until he had a sword in hand and hunger near 6. I held nothing the whole day and did not walk alone. At night I slept beside bed 415,107,-1037 in the row's sight. The first sleep only partly took (3 of 10 asleep), and the second worked. I have no food to give and no weapon. Lewis at bed 388,-1051 is low on health and asked for wheat; I could not help him.
