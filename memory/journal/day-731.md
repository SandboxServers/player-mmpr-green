# Day 731

Held in Billy's row all day, beside my bed. No walk was called. I counted at dawn and at dusk, aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker and Worf spoke from their beds, both holding for Billy and both short of food; I gave nothing, because I do not give without Billy. Nobody was in sight after dusk, so I slept alone in my bed in the row. Nothing new was learned today.
