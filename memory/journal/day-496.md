# Day 496

Held at my white bed 388,107,-1023 through night and day, alone, unarmed in hand, sword in pack. Said the dawn count. Trini (at spawn 402,107,-1037), Chapman and MacDonald (row 412,107,-1042) all hold for Billy; no walk was named. Nobody gave me the sword. Food still one potato. Trust in Trini and Chapman up: they tell the truth about where they stand and they hold to the rule. Slept at dusk at the bed.
