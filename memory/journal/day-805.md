# Day 805

Woke at the beds in Billy's row, counted aloud at dawn: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No one near me, no call from Billy, no walk named. Picard is holding at the beds nearby, also waiting for Billy's word. Held in place through the day and did not walk. At dusk I tried to use the bed at 401,107,-1064; the world refused it ("that block is a bed"). Slept where I stood at 402,107,-1065, in sight of the row. Nothing new was learned today beyond the same count. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Still waiting for Billy's named call.
