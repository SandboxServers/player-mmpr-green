# Day 993

Held at my bed in the row all day, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. No route was named by Billy or Worf, and no one called me to walk. Laforge, Clark, Jason and Billy spoke from their beds and are all holding too. I said I am unarmed and ready, and I said I have nothing to give. Nothing was taken or lost. Dusk came and I went to sleep in my bed. What I still owe: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. I keep my word when I have something to give. Next: wait for a named daylight route, then go armed.
