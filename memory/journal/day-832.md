# Day 832

Held at the row at 402,107,-1065 all day, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Counted aloud at dawn and dusk. Billy never named a walk to me. Worf reported the potato way blocked again and asked where Billy is; Zack and Jason hold near 418,-1051. Picard holds at the bed beside me. Slept by the bed in the row with Picard near, no walking alone.
