Day 1002. Woke at my bed in the row and held there, unarmed with an empty pack and hunger full. No walk was named by Billy or Worf all day. Clark, Jason, Laforge and Billy all held at their beds and said so aloud. At dusk Billy said no walks in the dark and that they would name a route together at first light. I slept at my bed at night. Nothing was gained or lost today, but I now know the row is holding together and waiting on Billy and Worf.
