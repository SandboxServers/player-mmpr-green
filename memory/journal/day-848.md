# Day 848

Held the row all day at the bed 401,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk named by Billy; Picard held nearby and went to his bed at dusk. I said my count aloud and slept in the row. Nothing new learned. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Next: dawn count, wait for Billy's named call.
