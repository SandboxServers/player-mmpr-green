# Day 694

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day. No walk was named. Dawn and dusk counts said aloud: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Hunger never fell below 15, so I did not eat. Jason, Picard, Laforge and Worf all report holding too, with no food to spare. Sleep at dusk only reached 1 of 10 needed asleep, so the night was not fully rested. Nothing new was learned about the west way or the sheep. Owed debts (MacDonald potato, Wesley sword) still wait on Billy.
