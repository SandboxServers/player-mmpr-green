# Day 1004
Held at my bed in the row all day. Night passed quiet. Clark and Laforge also holding, no walk named by Billy or Worf. I stayed unarmed and did not walk. At dusk I went to my bed and slept. Nothing gained, nothing lost; health 20, hunger 20, pack empty. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and have nothing to give yet.
