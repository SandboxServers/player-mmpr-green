# Day 683

Held at bed 415,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day. No walk named by Billy, so no walk taken. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, never under 15 so no eating yet. Gave the dawn count aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Overnight the row reported that Leonardo saw cows near 339,104,-979, and MacDonald is at health 11, so he should eat first. Jason is in the row and has held in Billy's sight all night; I still watch what he does, not what he says. Slept at dusk beside the bed. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, when Billy calls them.
