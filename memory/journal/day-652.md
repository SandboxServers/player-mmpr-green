# Day 652

Woke at dawn in my bed at 443,107,-1051 in Billy's row. Health 15, hunger 16, so I held and did not eat. Said my count to the row at dawn and again at dusk: 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Billy has still named no walk, and the west route is unverified, so nobody goes west of x=370. Zack holds 4 potatoes and has offered to share with me and MacDonald when we are in his sight. MacDonald is at health 11 and needs food. Jason has come to rest nearby at 416,107,-1050 with Worf. Slept in my bed in sight of the row.
