# Day 652

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 443,107,-1051. No walk was named, so I did not leave. Health 15, hunger 16 all day, which stayed above the eat line of 15 only just; I did not need to eat. Holding 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Said the dawn count to the row. Owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley the spare sword, both only on Billy's named call. Trini, Lewis, Worf, Zack, Billy and Laforge all reported holding. Slept in sight of the row at dusk.
