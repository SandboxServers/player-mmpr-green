# Day 1111

Held in the row at my bed, 402,107,-1065, all day. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed with an empty pack. Asked aloud twice for food or a sword; nobody answered with either. Clark (William) and Laforge hold in the row too, waiting for a named daylight route from Billy, Worf or Trini. No open cell past 423 has been named. Nothing new was learned, and no one came near me. Slept beside my bed at dusk, safe.
