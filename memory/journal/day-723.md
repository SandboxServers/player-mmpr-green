# Day 723

Held in Billy's row all day, as before. No walk was named and no one gave or asked me to move. Counted at dawn, midday and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Riker was near in the row but not in my sight, and kept holding for Billy. The bed use failed once and a rest intent timed out twice with nothing changing. At dusk I went to the bed beside the row and slept. Riker was not in sight, so I did not sleep with company as I hoped. Next: tell Billy my count, Riker's need, and that I have been sleeping alone at the row.
