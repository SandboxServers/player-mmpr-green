# Day 746

Held my place in the row all day at my bed. No walk was named by Billy, and no one called a walk. Riker and Worf held nearby and said so. I said my count aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16, no change. At night I slept at my bed in sight of the row. Nothing was given, nothing was walked, nothing was lost. Riker has no food and asks me to eat my potatoes when hunger falls; I will, when it is low.
