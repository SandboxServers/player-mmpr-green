Day 705. Counted at dawn in Billy's row: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No call from Billy all day, so I held my place and walked nowhere. Riker spoke from his bed: health 20, hunger 20, no food, waiting on Billy's named call too. I did not give anything. Hunger stayed at 16, above the eat line. At night I went to the bed one west of me and slept, sheltered in the row.
