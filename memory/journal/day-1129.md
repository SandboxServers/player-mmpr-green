# Day 1129

Woke at my white bed 402,107,-1065 in the row. Health and hunger stayed at 20 all day; still unarmed with an empty pack. I asked the row for a stick, food and a daylight route past 423. No one answered me directly. Laforge and Clark are still holding at their beds, waiting for Billy, Worf or Trini to name a checked route. Nothing was gained today. At dusk I went to bed beside the row.
