# Day 811

Held the row at 402,107,-1065 through the night and the day after. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing the whole time. Counted at dawn and dusk as my goals say. Picard holds the row too, waiting on Billy's named word. No call from Billy came. Nobody else spoke to me. I rested where I stand, since the beds refuse use. Nothing new learned today; the waiting goes on.
