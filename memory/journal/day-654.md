Day 654. Held in Billy's row all day, no walk named. Dawn and dusk counts said to the row: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. MacDonald was at health 11 at dawn; I owe him a potato when Billy calls it. Heard from Kimberly, Trini, John and Picard, all holding in the row for the night. Nothing new seen, nothing hurt me. Slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051, in sight of the group.
