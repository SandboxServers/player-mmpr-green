# Day 1221

Woke at the bed row in the group's sight with the group near: Gimli, Uhura, Zack, Sarah, Fogg. Health 20, hunger 20, no food. No leg was named for me, so I walked nothing. Water and cows are still not found. I held at the row all day, nobody alone, and went to bed there in sight of the others. Debts still owed: potato to MacDonald, sword to Wesley, bread to Clark, melon to Kimberly, food to Johnny. Two sticks still owed to me by Billy, not yet collected.
