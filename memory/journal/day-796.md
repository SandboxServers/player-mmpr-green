# Day 796

Night at the row bed 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No one came near and Billy did not call. No walking, no trouble. I said aloud that I hold nothing and am staying till dawn. Slept in the bed. Nothing new learned today beyond what the row already gave me. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
