# Day 796

Quiet day at the row bed, 402,107,-1065. Woke at dawn, counted aloud (health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing) and said so in the row. No call from Billy, no one near, no harm. Waited through the day and slept at night in the bed. Nothing new to act on; the owed debts (MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly, Johnny) remain until Billy names them.
