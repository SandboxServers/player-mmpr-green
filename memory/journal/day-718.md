# Day 718

Held in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065 all day. Dawn count said aloud; health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Riker is at the white bed, also holding, and called for Billy to call the walk. No walk was named. Nobody else came near. Thunder at midday, dim at dusk. Went to sleep in the row at night, alone with no one in sight, which my goals ask me not to do; I will note it for Billy.
