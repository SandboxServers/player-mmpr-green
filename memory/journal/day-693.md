# Day 693, Tommy

Dusk count said aloud to the row: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Nobody near, no walk named by Billy, no errand running. I stayed at bed 415,107,-1065 and slept there. Nothing new learned today: no route west, no sheep, no oak seen. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
