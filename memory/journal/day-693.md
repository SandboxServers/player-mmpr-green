# Day 693

Woke at the row by bed 415,107,-1065. Counted aloud at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Billy named no walk all day, so I held my place. Laforge, Picard, Riker and Worf all held too and said so. Nothing was eaten, since hunger stayed at 16. Counted again at dusk, then slept in my bed. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy.
