# Day 628, Tommy

Held in the row all day. Dawn count to Billy: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords. Billy called no west walk because bows were drawn to the southwest, and said to hold at the beds, eat at hunger 10, and feed the weakest first. MacDonald (health 7) is the one in need; Troi's potato went to Chapman by mistake and I offered MacDonald one of mine if he stood in my sight. Nothing was given. Slept at dusk in bed 429,107,-1037 from 430,107,-1037; sleep worked. Nothing new learned about the west way; it is still blocked at 413,107,-1050.
