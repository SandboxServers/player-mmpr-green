# Day 1036

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Said I was holding and that I owe debts I cannot pay yet. Laforge, Clark, Jason and Billy are all holding too. Nobody has named a daylight route past 423 with exact open cells, so I did not walk. Jason holds at 417,107,-1051 and names no route to melons. Billy is sleeping at 415,107,-1051. Night came; I slept in my bed. Nothing hostile came near. Still no food, no sword, no route.
