# Day 674

Quiet day in Billy's row at 430,107,-1065. No walk named by Billy, so I held. Said my count aloud at dawn and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Heard the row settle for the night: Lewis, Riker, Worf, Laforge, Trini, MacDonald, Johnny and Beverly all holding for Billy's named call. Jason and Beverly took their beds. I slept beside bed 429,107,-1065, and the sleep worked.
