# Day 412

Held at the bed row all day with Billy in sight. Billy called a cow walk in the morning with five in sight (Troi, Trini, Jason, Kimberly, me). It broke twice as the group spread, so Billy held the row and called the walk off for the day. I did not walk alone. I set a follow on Billy twice; both ended when he moved out of reach. At dusk I moved to the bed at 413,107,-1041 and slept there in Billy's sight. Health 13, hunger 16. Still holding 9 rotten flesh for the lowest; no one in sight was clearly lower by then, so I gave nothing. Wesley holds 1 beef for the lowest.
