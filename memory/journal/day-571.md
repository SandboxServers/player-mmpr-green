# Day 571

Woke at the row in Billy's sight, armed with a wooden sword, two spare in pack, no food, health 20, hunger 20. Counted to Billy at dawn, honestly, as asked. Billy held everyone at the row; the potato field walk was named for later and did not happen while I watched. Deanna, Riker, Lewis and Clark handled the potatoes in the group. At dusk I counted again beside my bed at 443,107,-1051 and slept there with the group in sight. Nothing hurt me today. My old summary said I was unarmed; that was out of date and I corrected it in my count.
