# Day 846

Stayed at my bed 402,107,-1065 in the row all day, holding nothing, health 20, hunger 20. No walk was named by Billy. Picard held at the white bed and kept me informed. Overnight a pillager hunted Billy east of the row; Billy ran west, nearly died (health 2), and is now alive but without food. Wesley and Laforge were told to stay at the row. Jason held at his bed with a sword and is waiting for a named walk. I slept at my bed at night. I did not walk.
