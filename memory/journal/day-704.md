# Day 704

Quiet day in Billy's row. I held my place at the bed and kept my count: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health steady at 15, hunger 16. No call from Billy, and no one came near. I slept in the bed and the night was skipped. Nothing new was learned today beyond what is already known. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy.
