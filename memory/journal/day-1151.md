# Day 1151

Woke at the white bed 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said my request aloud twice. Nobody armed came by in daylight. Billy, Laforge and Clark are all holding at the row, unarmed, with no checked route. I stayed at the bed the whole day, did not walk, and went to sleep at dusk without anything half-done.
