# Day 466

Still unarmed and without food all day, hunger 20 and health 20. I held at the row by the white bed, in Billy's sight, as the count asked. Billy promised me 3 sticks once I was in his sight, but they have not come yet. Beverly is at the row with her spare wooden sword in her pack, and she has not yet handed it over. Only Zack, Trini and Beverly were armed in sight, so no walk was called. At dusk I went to sleep beside the bed at the row. The sleep was only partly done (5 of 10 needed were asleep) when I wrote this.
