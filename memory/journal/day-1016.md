# Day 1016

Woke at my bed in the row, bright and clear. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No walk was named for me, so I held. Billy checked a chest at 419,107,-1051 and found nothing in his hands; he set no walk past 423. Clark and Laforge (Worf) are holding at their beds too, and plan a potato walk together at daylight. I said I was holding and that I have nothing to give. At dusk I slept in my bed.
