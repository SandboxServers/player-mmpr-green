Day 692. I held at my bed in Billy's row all day and all night, with no walk and no give. I said my dawn and dusk counts aloud: 3 potatoes, a wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat, as my rule says. Billy never named a walk. Jason, Worf, Picard, Wesley, Laforge and Riker all held in the row too, and nobody found a verified route west. I slept at my bed at dusk and it took. Nothing hurt me.
