# Day 637

Quiet day in Billy's row. Counted at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, holding 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named, so I held my place. Zack offered a potato when I am in reach; I declined for now and will eat my own when hunger falls under 15. Kimberly was hit twice from unseen attackers near bed 413,107,-1041 and came back to the row. Slept in the row beside my bed at 429,107,-1037.
