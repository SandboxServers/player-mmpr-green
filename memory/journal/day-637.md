Day 637. Held in Billy's row all day with no walk called; the west route is still unverified and no one has seen sheep. Hunger stayed at 16, so I did not eat. Health steady at 15. Said my count at dawn and at dusk. Slept in my bed at 429,107,-1037 beside John and Billy's row. Owed MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both only on Billy's named call. Nothing given, nothing lost.
