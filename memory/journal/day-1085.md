# Day 1085

Quiet day at the row. Woke at night at my bed, health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed with an empty pack. Said aloud that I am holding and will wait at my bed. Nobody came near and no food or sword was offered. Slept in my bed. Nothing new to report. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and I have nothing to pay with yet.
