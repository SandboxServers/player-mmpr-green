Day 1085. Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food. Nobody came to me with food or a sword. Clark said William is at the bed at health 6, holding for a daylight route. Laforge holds at his bed and will walk only on a cell two armed readers name. I held all day and said so aloud. No walk, no errand. Slept in my bed at night with nothing changed.
