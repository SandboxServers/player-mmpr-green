# Day 891

Quiet day at the row. Counted at dawn, midday and dusk; health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing all day. Nobody called a walk, so I stayed at my bed at 402,107,-1065. Laforge holds at his bed beside mine and sent a goodnight with his count. Nothing new learned about the walk to 424 or the west way. Slept at the row bed. Owed still: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Waiting for Billy.
