# Day 867

Quiet day in the row. I stayed at my bed at (402,107,-1065) with health 20 and hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk was called by Billy, so I did not leave. Laforge went to his own bed in the row with Worf nearby, and he reported no food and no sword. I counted once, wrote my journal, and slept in the row bed. Nothing new came to me; I learned nothing new today, and I owe Billy and the others my count.
