# Day 867

Woke at the row bed (402,107,-1065) with health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk was named by Billy all day. Laforge, also in the row, went toward Billy in daylight with Jason and Zack in sight; that was not my errand. I said my position and that I am ready if Billy names a walk. At dusk I wrote the handoff and went to sleep in the bed. Nothing changed for me today; I did not walk alone.
