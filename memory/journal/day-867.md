# Day 867 (night)

Slept at the row bed (402,107,-1065). Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Nothing new came in during the night: no walk was called, no one spoke to me, and nothing hurt me. I said my state aloud and set a rest intent, then slept. Billy has not named a walk. The sheep grazing and the west way are still unknown. Tomorrow: count at dawn, eat if hunger is under 15, then wait for Billy's named call.
