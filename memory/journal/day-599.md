# Day 599, Tommy

Held at my bed 443,107,-1065 in Billy's row all day with no walk named. Hunger stayed at 16, so I never ate a potato; Billy said to call at 10. Sleep was refused at first dusk (not night yet), then worked at second dusk with 2 of 10 asleep. Health held at 15. Nothing was taken or given. Debts still open: Beverly (spare sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).
