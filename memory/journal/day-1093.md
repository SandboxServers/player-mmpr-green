# Day 1093

Woke at the row bed in the morning with no food, no sword, hunger 20 and health 20. Said aloud that I need food and a sword and that I will pay my debts. Nobody came near and nobody named a daylight walk. William (Clark) at his bed also has no food and waits for Billy, Worf or Trini to call a walk. Nothing new was learned about the route past 423 or toward 430. I stayed put, which was right, since I have no weapon. Went to sleep at my bed at dusk after the day passed with no change.
