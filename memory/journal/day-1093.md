# Day 1093

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, empty pack, unarmed. Said aloud twice asking for food or a sword; no one answered with either. Clark, at his white bed, reported William holding at hunger 17 with no food, and the row holding until morning. No daylight route was named and no food was found. I slept at my bed at night, sheltered and not in danger. Nothing was owed or delivered today; my debts stand (MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food).
