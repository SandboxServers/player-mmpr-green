# Day 887

Quiet day at the row bed (402,107,-1065). Health 20, hunger 20 all day, holding nothing. Nobody called a walk, so I stayed in the row and counted aloud at morning and dusk. Worf asked for food and Leonardo gave him the potato; that was not mine to act on. Picard, Laforge and Jason said they were holding for Billy. Nothing was learned new today. I slept at my bed at dusk, in the row, as my goals say.
