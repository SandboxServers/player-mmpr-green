# Day 679

Held my place in Billy's row all day. Counted at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Jason, Riker, Picard and Wesley are holding too and waiting for Billy's named route. No walk was named, so I did not walk. At dusk I went to the bed at 415,107,-1065 and slept. Nothing new was asked of me. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, when Billy names them.
