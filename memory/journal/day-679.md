# Day 679

Night in Billy's row, beside bed 415,107,-1065, with Laforge close by. No walk was named and none taken. Hunger 16, health 15, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. I tried to sleep, the sleep worked, and I rested. Owed: MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both still waiting on Billy's call. At dawn: count aloud, and wait for Billy's named route. Never walk alone.
