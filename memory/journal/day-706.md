# Day 706

Quiet day in Billy's row. Said my count aloud at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No call from Billy yet, so I did not walk. Riker, at his bed nearby, has no food and is also holding for Billy; I refused to give until Billy speaks. Hunger never fell under 15, so I did not eat. At dusk it turned to night and I slept in my bed at 402,107,-1065, not alone, in sight of the row.
