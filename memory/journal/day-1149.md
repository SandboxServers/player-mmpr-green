# Day 1149

Woke at my bed 402,107,-1065, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody armed came near. I asked aloud for a weapon, food and my sticks. Wesley, Clark, Billy and Laforge all answered: they hold at the row and will not walk until a checked daylight route is named. Billy will view east at first light. At dusk I slept at the bed. Nothing new came in the night. Still no weapon, no food, no route.
