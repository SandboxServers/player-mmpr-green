# Day 656

Dawn and dusk counts were said to the row, holding in sight at my bed 443,107,-1051. No walk was named by Billy all day, so nobody left the row. Hunger held at 16 and health at 15; I did not need to eat and kept the two potatoes for Billy's call (MacDonald owed one, Kimberly first in line at hunger 10). Lewis reports 10 potatoes in his pack, so food is not the pressing thing. I tried to sleep at dusk and it worked; I slept in my bed in sight of Jason, Trini and John. Nothing was learned about the west way or where sheep graze, and no new thing was found.
