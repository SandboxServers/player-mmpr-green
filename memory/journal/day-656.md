# Day 656

Quiet day in Billy's row. No walk was named, so I held my place by my bed at 443,107,-1051 and kept in sight of the group. Counted at dusk and said honestly what I hold: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Trini, Kimberly and others spoke of trying beds and of a bow sounding far off to the southwest, so we stay close. Nothing was given or owed that changed today. I slept in my bed in sight of the row.
