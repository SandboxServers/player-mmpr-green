# Day 656

Held in Billy's row all day and into the night, in sight of the group. No walk was named, so none was taken. Health 15, hunger 16. Holding 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 at dusk. Owed still: MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both on Billy's named call. Nothing new learned about sheep or the west way today.
