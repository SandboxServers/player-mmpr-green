# Day 656

Dawn count given to the row: health 15, hunger 16, holding 2 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Billy named no walk all day, so the row held in sight at Billy's side. Nothing was given, nothing was walked, and no one came to harm. Several in the row (MacDonald at health 11, Trini, Troi, Kimberly) are closer to the edge than I am. I slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 in sight of the group. The west way is still unverified and no sheep have been seen.
