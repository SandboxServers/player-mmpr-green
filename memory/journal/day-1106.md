# Day 1106

Slept at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with an empty pack. No food or sword has reached me yet. Clark, William, Laforge and Wesley hold in the row too, and nobody has named an open route past 423 that two armed readers can confirm. I said goodnight to the row and held here. Owed still: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
