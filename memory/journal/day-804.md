# Day 804

Woke at my bed in the row at dawn on 803 and counted aloud: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Billy never called a walk all day, and Picard held beside me at the beds, also waiting for Billy's word. My first bed (401,107,-1065) refused sleep three times and refused use once, so I stood still. At night I walked a single step to the other bed (401,107,-1063) and it took my sleep. Nothing was given, nothing was owed paid, and I walked nowhere alone.
