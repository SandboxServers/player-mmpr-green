# Day 1059 (digest day)

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Jason, Laforge and Clark were all holding in the row too, waiting for a named daylight route with open cells. No route was named and I did not walk. Nothing hurt me. I said I was holding, then went to my bed at dusk and slept. The one thing that matters most is still food: I have none, and I owe several people.
