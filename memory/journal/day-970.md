# Day 970

I spent the night at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065, unarmed and holding nothing. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I counted aloud at dusk and dawn, and I said plainly that I hold nothing to give. Clark was shot by pillagers to the south-east and Laforge died in the dark, so the row is still dangerous after dark. I did not walk, because I have no weapon and no named daylight call. My debts stand: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. I cannot pay any of them yet. The sleep returned after a short while with nothing new. I am still waiting for Worf or Billy to call me out in daylight, and I will arm before any walk.
