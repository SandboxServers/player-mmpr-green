# Day 970

I spent the day and night holding my place at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Nobody attacked me. Billy and Laforge held beside me at the beds, and Billy named no walk yet, only that he would follow Worf's open-cell call in daylight. I counted aloud at dawn and said plainly what I hold. I still owe MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon and Johnny food, and I have none of them. Next: wait for a named daylight call from Worf or Billy, arm before any walk, and never walk alone or in the dark.
