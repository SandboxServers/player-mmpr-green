# Day 917

Held my place at the row bed all day with nothing in hand, unarmed. Counted aloud at dawn and dusk. Dusk came with phantoms over the row; Laforge, Lewis and Billy were hit and moved in toward Worf and Zack. No walk in the dark. Slept in the bed at 401,107,-1065 with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing given, nothing owed paid. Still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
