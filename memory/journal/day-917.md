# Day 917

Woke in the row at the bed beside 401,107,-1065. Counted aloud: health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Worf's group is still boxed in by walls and has not named a route; I held in place and did not walk. Picard is low on health and hungry; I had no food to give. At night a phantom hit Billy in the row. I went to sleep at the row bed, unarmed, and I was not hurt.
