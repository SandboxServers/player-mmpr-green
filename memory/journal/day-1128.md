# Day 1128

Woke at my white bed 402,107,-1065 in the row, unarmed, pack empty, health 20, hunger 20. Called out at morning for a stick, food, or a named daylight route past 423. No one answered me directly; I overheard Laforge and Clark, both still holding with no food or sword. Nothing new was learned about the route, and no food or weapon came to me. I stayed by the beds through the day and slept at dusk in the row. The debts still stand: MacDonald (potato), Wesley (spare sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny (food). Nothing in hand to pay with.
