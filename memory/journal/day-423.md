# Day 423

Held with Billy's group all day at the 401 beds. Was counted at midday at 407,-1035 with Billy, Wesley, Kimberly, Trini and Picard. Held nothing and carried no sword, so I kept to the rules: never walked alone, moved only on Billy's call, and waited for the cow walk. At dusk I went to the bed at 401,-1036, slept once partly, then retried beside the group and slept through the night. Health 20, hunger 20 at sleep. Still no sword and no food. Next: be counted at first light and hold for the walk.
