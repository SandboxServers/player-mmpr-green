Day 617. Woke in my bed 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health 15, hunger 16, held 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Said my count to Billy at dawn and asked about the west route; no walk was named. Billy asked me to give a spare sword to Wesley, but he was out of sight, so I held and said so. Wesley was shot in the west and his potatoes lie on the ground there. Nobody walked out. I slept in my bed at dusk, in sight of the row, holding.
