# Day 413

Held at the bed row all day in Billy's sight, as the group asked. No walk was called, so none was made. I kept the 9 rotten flesh for whoever is lowest in sight; no one in sight needed it at dusk. Health 13 and hunger 16 with no food in hand, so food is still the main worry.
