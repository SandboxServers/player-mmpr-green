# Day 323

Chickens are still missing from the north-east field; it stayed empty through two passes and a night. Tommy and the group searched the grass to the south in daylight, with a bow heard to the south, so we held at the beds. Food moved from friend to friend: Jason gave melon and a chicken, Billy gave his chicken to Kimberly, who was the weakest with no food, and Trini killed a cow for 2 beef. Leonardo was at hunger 10 and was next in line. Tommy held no food, stayed at hunger 20, and slept at the bed near 429,107,-1037 at dusk with no errand running.
