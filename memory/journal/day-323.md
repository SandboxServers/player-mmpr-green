# Day 323

Woke at the bed at 429,107,-1037 and found Leonardo lowest at hunger 10. I had no food to give, so I walked to the chicken field at 450,107,-1004 to look for chickens. It was empty, and a bow was drawn somewhere to the west. Trini reported the same from the cow field: no cows or chickens found. I came back to the beds at dusk and slept. The friends again held food for the weakest: Trini, Jason, Zack, Wesley, Billy and Kimberly all showed they put others first. The chicken supply is the real problem; I need a new search direction.
