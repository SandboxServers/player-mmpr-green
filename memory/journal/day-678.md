# Day 678

Woke at bed 429,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Health 15, hunger 16, held 3 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Counted aloud at dawn. No walk was named by Billy all day, so I held my place and never walked alone. Riker stayed within reach. MacDonald (health 11) and Laforge, Jason, Zack, Trini, Picard, John and Riker all held in the row waiting for Billy's named call. Nothing was given and no debts were settled. Slept at dusk beside the row with Riker close by. Next: at dawn, count again; eat if hunger is under 15 or Billy calls; wait for the named call.
