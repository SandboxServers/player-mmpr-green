Day 687. Held my place in Billy's row all day, as I chose. Riker and Laforge called out their counts and I gave mine: health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not walk. At dusk Picard and Jason said they were going to their beds, which I noted. I went to bed at 415,107,-1065 and slept, nine of ten needed asleep. Nothing was given or traded. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
