# Day 534

Held at the row in Billy's sight all day, unarmed, health 20, hunger 20, no food. Counted at dawn 533 and 534. Billy named no walk. The sword I was next for did not come: Data had given his away to Jason, and Billy has no spare. Riker tried the wooden sword beside Data in daylight and it would not go into hand. I asked Billy where my sword is; he said to hold and he would name the next one when one frees. Slept at my bed 401,107,-1022 at dusk 534, beside the group. Nothing was lost. Nothing was gained. The lesson: a sword that is promised is not in hand until it is.
