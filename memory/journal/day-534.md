# Day 534

Dusk at the row beside Zack. Billy named no walk and no spare sword; he said the count stays at four armed. I stayed by the beds in sight of the group, unarmed, no food, health 20. Billy's group: Picard, Jason, Data, Riker, Laforge, MacDonald and others armed or holding. Picard and Jason asked for beds. At night I slept by the bed at 401,107,-1022 beside Zack and woke with six of ten asleep. Nothing was given to me. Still no sword in my hand.
