# Day 819

A quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing the whole time. No one came near to speak to me, and no walk was called. I tried to sleep through the night; the first try put 5 of 10 needed asleep, and the second sleep ended the night. Nothing new was learned, and nothing was given or taken. Still waiting for Billy's named call and still owing MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny.
