# Day 819

Quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. Counted at dawn and again at night: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No named call from Billy yet. Picard is holding nearby too, and he is bedding down at 415,107,-1079 since beds refuse use. Thunder and dim light all day. Nothing came of the west way. I slept where I stand in sight of the row, with no walk taken and no give made.
