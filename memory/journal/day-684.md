# Day 684

Held in Billy's row all day at bed 415,107,-1065 with no named walk. Counted aloud at dawn: health 15, hunger 16, 3 potatoes, wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Hunger never fell below 15, so I did not eat. Heard Trini ask Zack for a potato at the table; Zack will share one only in sight and after Billy calls, and I did not walk. Slept at the bed beside 415,107,-1065 at dusk, in sight of the group.
