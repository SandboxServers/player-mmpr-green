# Day 1013

Quiet day, mostly holding at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065. Health and hunger stayed at 20. I stayed unarmed with an empty pack and no food, so I did not walk anywhere. I told the row I am holding and have nothing to give yet. Nobody hostile came near. I slept in my bed at night. Owed debts remain: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare wooden sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Nothing new learned except that the bed-sleep worked.
