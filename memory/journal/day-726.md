# Day 726

Held my place in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065 all day, bright and clear. No walk was named, so I did not walk and did not give. Said my count at midday and again on 725: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16, so I did not eat. Riker and Worf stayed at their beds nearby, Riker still short on food and still waiting on Billy. Nobody was in sight through the night of 724 and 725, so I did not sleep alone. At dusk on 726 Riker and Worf were near, and I slept in the bed beside me. Nothing hurt me and nothing was lost.
