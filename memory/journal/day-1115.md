# Day 1115

Held at my white bed in the row, 401,107,-1065, the whole day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. I asked Billy, Trini and Zack for food or a sword and got no answer from them. Clark (William), Laforge, Wesley and Worf are all holding too, with no food. Worf will not spare his sword. The row chest at 405,107,-1065 came back empty when I opened it. No route past 423 has been named, so nobody walks. I went to bed at dusk and slept at the row bed.
