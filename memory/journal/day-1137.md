# Day 1137

Woke at my white bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. Called aloud for a stick, a sword and food. No one answered my call. Clark (William) and Laforge both hold at their beds, unarmed, with no daylight route named; Laforge will not walk until two armed readers name a cell. I held too and walked nowhere. At dusk I wrote a handoff, slept at my bed, and the night was skipped.
