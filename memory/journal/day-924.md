# Day 924

Quiet day in the row at 402,107,-1065. Counted at dawn and midday: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing, unarmed. No walk was named by Worf, so I held my place. Went to bed in the row bed at night; nobody was near. Nothing new learned. Debts still owed: MacDonald potato, Wesley spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
