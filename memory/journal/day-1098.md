# Day 1098

Dusk at my bed in the row, about 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. No food and no sword came to me today. I told the row I would hold until daylight and ask Worf and Trini for food first. Clark and William are holding there too, also unarmed and short of food. Nothing new was learned about routes past 423 or toward 430. I slept at my bed when night came.
