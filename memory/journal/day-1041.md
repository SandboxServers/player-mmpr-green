# Day 1041

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing hostile came near. Said I am holding. Billy, Clark and Laforge all agreed: no walk until two readers name a cell, and 420 stays shut. Nobody named me for a route, so I did not walk. Owed debts stand: MacDonald potato, Wesley sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. I have nothing to give yet. Day passed quietly; slept in my bed at dusk.
