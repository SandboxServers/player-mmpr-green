# Day 937

Counted at dawn and again at night at my bed in the row. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No walk was named by Worf, so I held my place. A sleep attempt at dusk failed because it was not yet night; a later sleep at the bed passed the night with nothing new. Nothing came in while I waited. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Nothing new was learned today, and no one new was met.
