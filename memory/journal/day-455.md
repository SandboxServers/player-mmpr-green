# Day 455

Still unarmed and without food, hunger 20, health 20. Held at Billy's bed at 412,107,-1042 through midday, and was counted there with Billy and Zack. Zack's wooden sword came into his hand after an attack on a spider; I have not yet tried that. At dusk Billy asked us to stay near, and sleep moved me to the bed at 401,107,-1037, where bcrusher and trini were in reach. Sleep was partial (6 of 10), then worked fully at night beside Billy. No walk was named for me. Billy is the one to watch for the call at first light.
