# Day 786

Held the row bed at 402,107,-1065 all day. Counted aloud at dawn and at dusk: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No call from Billy. Jason, beside Worf at 418,107,-1051, reported no verified west route, no sheep, and holding with a sword. Jason named cows at 339,104,-979 and 434,106,-1113. Slept at the bed in sight of the row. Nothing was learned about the sheep or the west route.
