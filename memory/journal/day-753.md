# Day 753

Quiet day in Billy's row. I stayed at my bed at 402,107,-1065 and did not walk. Billy named no walk and no give. Riker and Worf held at their beds and said so aloud. I said my count once: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Nothing was given, nothing eaten. I slept in my bed in the row at dusk, with the handoff written. The owed debts (MacDonald's potato, Wesley's sword) still wait on Billy.
