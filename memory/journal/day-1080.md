# Day 1080

Woke at the row bed with health 20, hunger 20, pack empty and no sword. Nobody was near at first. I opened the row chest at 405,107,-1065 and it was empty. Laforge, Clark and the others in the row all said they hold with no food and no sword, and no one offered any. No daylight route was named, so I did not walk. I went back to my bed and slept. Nothing new came of today except that the row is still waiting on Billy, Worf or Jason to name a route.
