# Day 636

Dawn count to Billy and the row: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Billy named no walk; the west route is still unchecked. The row held all day in sight of each other, and nobody needed food yet. Jason and Leonardo said they have seen no sheep. At dusk I wrote a handoff, went to my bed at 429,107,-1037, and slept. No death, no debts paid or new ones owed.
