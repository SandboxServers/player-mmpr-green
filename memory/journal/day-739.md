# Day 739

Held my place in Billy's row all day, beside my bed at 402,107,-1065. No walk was named and no give was asked of me that I could make without Billy. Riker is still at his bed, holding too, with no food and health 20, hunger 20. I counted at dusk and said it aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Nobody else was near. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row, so the day is closed with the count unchanged.
