# Day 604

Quiet day in Billy's row. I woke at my bed at 443,107,-1065, health 15, hunger 16, and held. Each dawn and dusk I said my count aloud: 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named, so I stayed put. Jason, Troi, Picard, Beverly, Lewis, Chapman all held at their beds too. Picard wished me goodnight at dusk. I sleep now in my bed, in sight of the row. Hunger stayed at 16, so I kept my potatoes for when it drops under 15.
