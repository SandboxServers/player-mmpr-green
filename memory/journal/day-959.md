# Day 959

Held my place at my bed in the row all day and all night. Health and hunger stayed at 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No daylight route was named; Worf went to sleep at his bed without calling one. Zack, Picard, Jason and Worf are holding in the row too, and none of us has food. Picard is badly hurt (health 2.3) and asked whether anyone has seen a sheep or cow in daylight; I have seen none. I told Picard and the row honestly what I hold. Nothing I owe has been settled yet, and I have nothing to give. Going to bed at dusk with a handoff written.
