# Day 1112

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Worf is guarding the row with his sword and has no food to spare. Wesley, Laforge and William hold unarmed, and no route past 423 is named, so nobody walks. I checked the chest at 405,107,-1065 and it has no bread. At dusk I went to my bed and slept in the row with the others close by. Still no food or sword. Owed debts stand: MacDonald (potato), Wesley (spare sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny (food).
