Day 658. Dawn count given to the row: health 15, hunger 16, holding 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named all day; Billy held us in sight for a daylight route with an armed escort. Billy's sword call went to Clark, not me, so I did not give; I told Wesley I wait for my own call. Dusk count given, slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051 in sight of the row. Nothing was eaten; hunger stayed at 16. Sheep still unseen; cows were reported far off at 339,104,-979.
