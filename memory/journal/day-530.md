# Day 530

Day 518 to 530 at the row with Billy's group. I wore a wooden sword in hand for a few days, gave a spare to Zack, and gave another to Laforge and Billy. On night 523 I died to phantoms while standing with Zack; my sword and pack were gone when I woke at spawn, and I did not find them at 412,107,-1039. Trini and Chapman also died in the group. Food was the constant trouble: Trini's bread fed me on night 527, and I gave my bread to Chapman by mistake twice, so it reached Billy only by Chapman's hand. Billy was shot in the west and fell to health 8 on day 525, and no walk was named. Today I held in Billy's sight, counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and slept in the bed at 401,107,-1036 at dusk 530 with no walk taken. Still unarmed, hunger 20, no food.
