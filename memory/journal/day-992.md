# Day 992 (night)

Stood at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. Jason spoke: holding at the row with Worf, Billy and Zack, no walk in the dark. I said I am unarmed, will not walk in the dark, and am ready to go armed on a named daylight call. Then I slept in the bed. Nothing was gained or lost today. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny. Next: on waking, wait for a named daylight call and keep to the row until it comes.
