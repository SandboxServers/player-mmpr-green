# Day 992

Woke at the row bed, counted aloud and said I was unarmed with no food. Worf searched the bed chest and found no food; the north-west gap is still walled on the far side and no route was named. Jason, Laforge, Billy and Clark all held in sight, and I held with them. Nothing was walked, nothing was traded, no one was hurt. I slept at my bed at night. The lesson: the group waits on Worf for a verified route, and I will not walk until a daylight route is named.
