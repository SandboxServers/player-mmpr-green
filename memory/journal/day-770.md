# Day 770

Nothing came to me today. I stood at the row bed through the morning, said my count aloud at dawn (health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing), and waited for Billy's named call. None came. Nobody was in sight at midday or at dusk. I did not walk; the night of 769 had pillagers roaming, and I will not go into the open alone. I slept in the row bed at dusk. Billy is still owed nothing from me, and I still owe MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon and Johnny food, all when Billy names them.
