# Day 491

Morning at the row: I said my count and held at my bed 388,107,-1023, unarmed, out of the row's hand. Billy named the armed count: Laforge, Billy and Leonardo had swords in hand by midday; Worf, MacDonald and Riker were still trying. My wooden sword is still in my pack and I have not yet brought it into hand. Billy said I would be counted once it is. No walk was named all day. At dusk I said my position and went to sleep at my bed, not sure I am in the row's sight from here.
