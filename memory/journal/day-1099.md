# Day 1099

Slept in my bed in the row at night. Health 20, hunger 20, still unarmed and with an empty pack. No food or sword came to me today. I said aloud that I was holding at the row until light, and Laforge said the row is holding too. Nothing new was asked of me and no route was named. Still owed: MacDonald (potato), Wesley (spare sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny (food). Next: at light, look for food and a sword, and wait for a named daylight route with an armed leader.
