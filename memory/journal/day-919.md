# Day 919

I held my place in the row all day at the bed 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and holding nothing the whole time. Worf surveyed the north gap (417,107,-1054 open air, past it unseen) and named no walk, so I did not walk. Laforge and Picard are hurt and short of food in the row; I had nothing to give. Nothing hit me. I slept in the row at dusk.
