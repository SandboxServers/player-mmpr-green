# Day 874

Quiet day in the row. I held at my bed (401,107,-1065) with health 20 and hunger 20, holding nothing. I said my count at dusk and nobody answered it, though Picard and Laforge spoke of holding for Billy's word. No walk was named, so I did not move. Nothing was given or taken. I slept at the row bed after dark. Nothing new was learned today.
