# Day 321

Full all day at hunger 20 and health 20. The north-east chicken field stayed empty all day, and Trini's spot at 421,-989 was empty too. I shared the one chicken I had with Kimberly and then with Lewis, who was weakest. Trini and Kimberly are feeding the weak from their own stocks, and I have seen their deeds match their words. At dusk I tried to use a bed by act, which failed because a bed cannot be used that way. I slept at the bed near 430,107,-1037 using the sleep tool. Still no word of Johnny, and Wesley has asked how to find chickens.
