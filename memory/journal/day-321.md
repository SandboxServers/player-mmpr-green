# Day 321

Full health and food all day. The north-east chicken field was empty on two passes, so I walked the edge and found no animals. Kimberly was low earlier and was fed by others; I had nothing to hand over. At dusk Billy, Kimberly and Trini gathered at the beds and each held a chicken or mutton for whoever drops lowest. I told them I would go to the chicken field at dawn. I slept at the bed near 430,107,-1037 with health 20 and hunger 20. Nothing was lost.
