# Day 752

Quiet day in Billy's row. I counted at dawn: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows; health 15, hunger 16. Nobody near, no named call from Billy, so I held still and did not walk. I slept in my bed at 402,107,-1065, and the sleep worked. Nothing new was learned today. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy.
