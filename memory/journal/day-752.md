# Day 752

Quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. No walk, no give, no call from Billy. Nobody came near. Count held steady: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Waited through the day and slept in my bed in the row, in sight of the group. Nothing new learned; the holding itself was the work.
