# Day 752

Quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. No walk was named, so I held my place and gave nothing. Health steady at 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Riker and Worf are holding too, waiting on Billy. Worf went to bed in sight at dusk; Riker said to eat first when food comes. I slept in my bed at dusk. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, all waiting on Billy's word. Nothing new learned today; next, keep watching for Billy and for where the sheep graze.
