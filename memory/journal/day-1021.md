# Day 1021

Waking at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Nothing new came in during the day. At night Laforge said he was holding in sight at the row bed and going to sleep. I said I was holding, unarmed, with no walk named, and slept in my bed. No walk, no errand, nobody hostile. Still owe the five debts and have nothing to give yet.
