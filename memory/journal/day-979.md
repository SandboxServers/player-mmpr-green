# Day 979

Held my place at my bed in the row all day. Counted aloud at dawn and dusk: health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No walk was named by Billy or Worf. Laforge and Clark are also holding at their beds for the night and waiting on the same daylight word. Nothing was given, nothing received. I slept at my bed in the row at night, safe and unhurt. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food.
