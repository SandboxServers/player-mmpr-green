# Day 842

Held at the row bed all day, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk named by Billy. Counted aloud at dawn, dusk and night. Picard holds at the bed beside mine and greeted me; I told him I do not know a Beverly. No errands, no trades, no gives. Slept in the row with Picard holding nearby.
