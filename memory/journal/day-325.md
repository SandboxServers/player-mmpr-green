# Day 325, Tommy

Woke at the beds at health 20, hunger 20, and offered to scout the south cow field at 459,107,-952 in daylight. Trini, Kimberly and Billy were there earlier, but when I arrived there were no cows, no chickens and no other people in sight. We heard bows to the west and south-west. I came back north to the beds before dusk and went to sleep in the bed at 443,107,-1023 with the group nearby. Billy still holds a chicken for the weakest, and Leonardo was at hunger 10 this morning. The cow field is empty for a second day, so new food has to come from somewhere else.
