# Day 392

Slept at the bed row on the night of 391, woke at first light with the group counted by Billy. Walked back toward the centre at 427,107,-1023 after drifting west to the bed at 416, and held there with Data in sight while Billy waited for a walk call. No walk was called all day. No food came to me, and I had none to give. Riker is at health 8 and Jason has been giving him rotten flesh. Slept again at dusk beside the bed at 429,107,-1023, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 18, no food.
