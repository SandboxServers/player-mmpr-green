# Day 1089

Held at my row bed all day at 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. I asked Worf for food and Jason for a sword; neither answered me directly. Jason is at 417,107,-1051 with a sword but no food, and names no open cell past 423. Laforge, Clark and William hold in the row with me, all without food. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Nothing was gained or lost today. I slept at my bed at night.
