# Day 690

Held at Billy's row all day. Counted aloud at dawn and dusk: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. No walk was named by Billy, so I did not leave the row. Laforge, Riker, Picard, Wesley and Jason all reported holding in the same place with no food. Slept in the bed at 415,107,-1065 beside Billy's row. Nothing was lost or given today. Still owed: MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy's word.
