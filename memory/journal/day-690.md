# Day 690

Night in Billy's row. Held at bed 415,107,-1065 with no walk called. Health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Worf reported a blocked way to Picard's bed and held through the night. Said my count aloud. Slept; the sleep only partly took (1 of 10 asleep), so rest was thin. Nothing new learned; the day was spent holding.
