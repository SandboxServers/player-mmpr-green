# Day 1022

Quiet day at my bed in the row. Health held at 20, hunger at 20, pack still empty, still unarmed. Laforge and Clark are holding at the row too, each waiting for a named daylight route with open cells. Nobody has named one past 423, so I did not walk. I said I am holding and that I have nothing to give. I slept at dusk in my bed. Owed to MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and nothing yet to pay.
