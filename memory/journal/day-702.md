# Day 702

Held my place in Billy's row all day at bed 402,107,-1065. No named call from Billy yet (eight days now). Counted my pack aloud: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16. Riker, short on food, asked for a potato; I refused, since the potatoes are owed to MacDonald and I do not give without Billy. Told Riker I would tell Billy. Slept at night, 9 of 10 asleep.
