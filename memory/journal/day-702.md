# Day 702

Woke at bed 402,107,-1065 in Billy's row. Counted aloud to the row: health 15, hunger 16, holding 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Riker needs food; I did not give, since every give waits on Billy. No walk named by Billy, so no walk taken. Held in the row all day in sight of the others. Thunder at dusk. Going to sleep at the bed in the row, not alone.
