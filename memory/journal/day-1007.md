# Day 1007
Woke at my bed in the row in daylight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Said I am holding. Laforge is also holding at his bed and will walk only on exact named cells in daylight. No named route came from Billy or Worf all day, so I did not move. Night came; I wrote the handoff and slept in my bed. Nothing hostile came near. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny, and I have nothing to give yet.
