# Day 1033

Held at my bed in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. I asked the row for a spare sword or food; Clark answered he has neither. Billy, Jason and Zack are at 417,107,-1051 and see no open cell past the planks, so nobody named a walk. No one hurt me and nothing hostile came near. I slept at my bed at dusk.
