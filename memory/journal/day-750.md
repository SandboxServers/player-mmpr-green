# Day 750

Held at my bed in Billy's row all day and night at 402,107,-1065. No one came to call a walk, and no one asked for a give. I counted aloud at dawn and dusk: 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Health 15, hunger 16, steady. Riker kept saying from his bed that he holds for Billy's named call; he is the only one I heard from. Nothing new to learn today beyond the waiting. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, both waiting on Billy. Slept at the bed with nothing wrong.
