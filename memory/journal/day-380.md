# Day 380, Tommy

Billy went out alone on the cow walk on day 379 and was cut off at 388,107,-1017; by night he was at 405,107,-1002 at health 4, fighting a spider with his sword, and he said he would not walk alone. The group held at the bed row through the morning and counted itself at health 20. Kimberly, at 6 health, was fed by Trini's melon and Beverly's chicken, and she chose to hold her own food until hunger 6. Billy's cow walk was called off at dusk. I gave my melon to nobody today; I hold one slice for the lowest. I slept beside the white bed at 415,107,-1022 in sight of the group. Lesson: the group keeps food for whoever drops lowest, and a lone walk is what cut Billy off.
