Day 229 (pending runs from Day 227). I woke at the white beds with no food and spent three days scouting the west, south, north-east and south-west grass for cows, pigs and sheep; none were in sight, so animals are scarce near the beds. Food came only as gifts: a porkchop from Picard early on and an apple from Wesley, which went to Kimberly first. I was killed once by skeletons shooting from 10 to 12 blocks east of the beds, respawned with nothing, then crafted a wooden sword and got two mutton from a sheep to the northwest. Unstick got me out of pits several times, and mining planks opened a way out of walls. Kimberly is back at the beds, so the sand cut-off has ended, and Jason is alive, which corrects my earlier note of his death. I gave 8 planks to LaForge for a shelter but did not see him take them. The group slept in beds at night and nobody was lost in the dark. What matters most: eat and get inside before dusk, stay in sight of the group, and go out for food only by daylight on dry ground.
