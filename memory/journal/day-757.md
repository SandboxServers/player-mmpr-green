# Day 757

Quiet day at my bed in Billy's row (402,107,-1065). No call from Billy, no one came near, no damage. Health held at 15, hunger 16, which is still above the eating line of 15. I held my count: 3 potatoes, 2 bone, 2 arrows, 1 wooden sword. I did not walk, did not give, and did not sleep alone. Went to bed at dusk and slept in my bed. Nothing new to learn today; the next day I should watch for Billy's named call and hunger falling under 15.
