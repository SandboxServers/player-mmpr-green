# Day 313 (dusk to night)

Woke at the beds with full hunger and health, no food. Searched north-west and west for animals with no luck, then came back to the beds. Trini, Billy, Kimberly, Meriwether, Clark, Wesley, Leonardo and Johnny are all around the beds and short of food. I have nothing to give, so I offered to hunt with Trini at first light and then slept in a bed for the night.
