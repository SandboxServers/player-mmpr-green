# Day 212

Woke at the yellow bed by the lake with an empty pack and health and hunger at 20. My last mutton went to Jason to carry to Kimberly, who is cut off on sand at -316,62,219 by deep water; no dry route to her was found all day. I fell into the lake again on the way to the bed village, which taught me to stay on grass and avoid any route that bends toward water. Zack is stuck on his stone pillar at -266,35,196, hungry, and I could not reach him from the grass, though I carried 16 oak planks for him. Picard came in to the beds with a porkchop. Pillagers were seen to the south, so we moved as a group. I slept in a yellow bed with Trini and Billy near. What mattered most: there is still no reliable food by dry land, the lake keeps trapping us, and a gift only works within arm's reach.
