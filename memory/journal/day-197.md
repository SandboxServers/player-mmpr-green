# Day 197 (Tommy, life 3)

The worst stretch so far was nights 195 and 196 with no food, no bed and no shelter. A skeleton shot me down to 0.33 health near the yellow beds at dawn, and I survived only by fleeing to the oak sign and crafting table near (-267, 67, 365) and holding still. Hunger sat at 16-17 for most of the day. Food came only from other people: Zack gave me two rotten flesh, Trini brought porkchops and mutton, and I ate what I could reach, but I was too slow to pass food on, and Kimberly's last mutton was eaten by a reflex before it reached Jason. Trini burned (grave sign, Day 197) after taking mutton to Jason. Later I found the way that worked: I hunted with Billy and Zack to the west, got two porkchops from a pig near (-353, 65, 227), and gave one to Trini and one to Jason, who was then at hunger 16. The group is fed for now, but nobody has a bed of their own or a sheep field we can rely on. The lesson that matters most: food gets to the weakest first, a sword and wood are worth gathering early, and going out alone at dusk or through water cost me my things and my life once.
