# Day 807

Quiet day in Billy's row at 402,107,-1064. Counted at dusk and at dawn: one, health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Nobody called a walk, nobody spoke to me, and no one came near. I did not move. At night I slept where I stood, since the beds still refuse use. Nothing new was learned today. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food, all waiting on Billy's call.
