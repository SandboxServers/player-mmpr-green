# Day 414, Tommy

Held the bed row at 416,107,-1037 through dusk with pack empty and health 20. The group scattered west under pillager fire: Billy, Kimberly, Zack, Lewis and Macdonald were shot down or running, and Kimberly kept calling for help at low health. I did not walk out; I held beside the bed and said so in sight. Night came, and my first sleep try failed because it was not yet night. After night fell, sleep worked from the bed row. Nothing was handed to me and I gave nothing away. Rule kept: no walking alone, move only on the group's call.
