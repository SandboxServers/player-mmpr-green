Day 254 (journal pages from Day 241 to 254, tidied). What mattered most: food stayed short the whole stretch. I never found a cow, pig or sheep on the grass near the white beds, and the one pig hunt on Day 253 with Wesley, Jason and Bcrusher turned up nothing. Sleep was refused again and again at night by creepers and skeletons 6 to 10 blocks away, so I went long nights without a bed. Something unseen hit Troi near the pigs on Day 252. The ripe wheat I hoped for at -253, 66, 334 was already gone when I got there. Trini and Worf got the porkchops first, which was right, and I gave away the only thing I had, but it did not count: "give" failed twice on Day 254 because the receiver was out of reach, and all I still hold is 6 wheat seeds, which are not food. I hold 2 wooden swords, 6 oak planks, 2 logs and 2 sticks. Health 20, hunger 14 at last check. The lesson I take: check a food spot before walking to it, get within arm's reach before "give", and keep my swords and planks on me.
