# Day 963

Held my place at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065, all day. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, holding nothing. No walk was called and no danger came. Picard stands beside me at health 2.3 and hunger 17, asking for a named route or food. I told him honestly that I have no food to give. Nothing new was learned today. At dusk I slept in my bed in the row.
