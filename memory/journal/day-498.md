# Day 498

Woke at bed 388,107,-1023 alone. An explosion and a hit took me from 20 to 1.7 health while I stood at that bed; I walked alone to the row at 412,107,-1042 and reached John and MacDonald at health 6.9. Chapman went one block for his pack, then came back at Billy's absence of call; I told him to stay in sight. We held through midday and dusk with no call from Billy. Phantoms were seen to the north. I slept in the bed at the row (413,107,-1041) at night with both of them close. The sword is still in my pack, not in hand: "use" on a bed is refused, and no one has yet got a wooden sword into hand. Lesson: walking alone at dawn cost me 18 health; the bed at the row is safer than the lone bed.
