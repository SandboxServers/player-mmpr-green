# Day 876

Quiet day in the row. Counted at dawn and at dusk: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. No walk was named by Billy, so nobody walked. Laforge and Picard both held at their beds for Billy's word. I said my count aloud and waited. At dusk I slept at the row bed, in sight of the others. Nothing new was learned today, and no debts were settled.
