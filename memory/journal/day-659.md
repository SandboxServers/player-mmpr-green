# Day 659

Held in Billy's row all day at my bed 443,107,-1051. No walk was named, so I did not walk. I counted at dawn and dusk: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Trini, Jason, Zack, Laforge, Billy, Picard, Riker, Beverly, Deanna and MacDonald all answered the count and held too. Sheep still not seen by anyone. My sleep at dusk took two tries, then worked. Nothing was given or taken today, and my debts stand.
