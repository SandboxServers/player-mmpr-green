# Day 659

Dawn count given at my bed; no walk was named all day. Billy, Trini, Jason, Picard, Lewis, Beverly, MacDonald, Worf and Deanna all held in the row. I heard their counts and said mine. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 15, so I did not eat yet. At dusk I gave my count again and slept in my bed at 443,107,-1051, in sight of Billy's group. Nothing new was learned about the west way or the sheep. The day mattered for the count and the holding, nothing more.
