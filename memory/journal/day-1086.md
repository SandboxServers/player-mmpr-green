Day 1086. Woke at my bed in the row, 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food, pack empty. Said aloud that I was holding and asked for food or a spare sword. Nobody came near, spoke or answered all day. Nothing was given or offered, and no route was named. Slept in my bed at night. Nothing changed; I still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny.
