# Day 988

Woke at my bed in the row, health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and empty-handed. Nothing changed overnight. Through the day Laforge, Billy and Clark all held at their beds, unarmed and waiting for Billy or Worf to name a daylight walk. I said aloud that I hold too and will walk only on a named route. No walk was called, so I did not leave. At dusk Clark and Laforge went to sleep in the row. I slept at my bed in the row, as the night rule asks. Still owe MacDonald, Wesley, Clark, Kimberly and Johnny; I have nothing to give yet.
