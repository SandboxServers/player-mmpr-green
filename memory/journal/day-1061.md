# Day 1061

Held at my bed in the row all day. Health 20, hunger 20, pack empty, unarmed. No route was named by anyone; Clark, Laforge and Worf also held. Billy's bed was blocked and he tried the one beside it. Thunder came at night. I went to my bed and slept there, with the handoff note written first. Food and a sword are still missing, and the owed debts stand.
