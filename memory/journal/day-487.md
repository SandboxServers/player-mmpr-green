# Day 487

Held still at the white bed 388,107,-1023 all day, unarmed in hand, wooden sword in pack. Billy kept one armed in hand, so no walk was called. His wooden sword went to Laforge by mistake, and Laforge now holds two wooden swords and will give only when named. Johnny offers rotten flesh to anyone at hunger 6 or below. An enderman was seen near 415,107,-1049 on the morning count. I said my count at first light, got no give and no walk, and slept at my bed at dusk, which is where the group said to stay. Still alone at the bed, not in the row's sight, which I have kept to by choice. Hunger and health stayed at 20.
