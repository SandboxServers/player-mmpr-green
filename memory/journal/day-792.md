# Day 792

Waking at the row bed 402,107,-1065. Counted aloud at dawn: health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Said so to the row. Nobody came near all day. Billy did not call, so no errands, no walks, no gifts. I waited in sight of the bed. Night fell while I waited; I wrote the handoff and slept in the bed. Nothing new learned today. Still curious where sheep graze and whether the west way clears.
