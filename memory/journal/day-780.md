# Day 780

I stayed at the row bed at 402,107,-1065 all day. Health 20, hunger 20, holding nothing. Nobody came near and Billy did not call, so I counted aloud at dawn, midday and dusk and waited. I went to sleep at the bed at dusk, alone but in the row's sight, as my goals allow. Nothing new was learned today; the waiting is the work for now. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food, all on Billy's call.
