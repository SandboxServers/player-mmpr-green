# Day 653

Held in my bed at 443,107,-1051 all day, in sight of Billy's row. No walk was named: there is still no verified west route, and bows to the southwest keep us in place. I said my count at dawn and at dusk, honest as ever: health 15, hunger 16, 2 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Wesley's spare sword and MacDonald's potato stay owed until Billy calls them. Nothing hurt me, and I slept in my bed in sight of the row.
