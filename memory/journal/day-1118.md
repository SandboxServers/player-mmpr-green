# Day 1118

Stayed at my white bed in the row all day, unarmed and without food, hunger still 20. Called out for food or a sword to Billy, Trini, Zack and Jason; none came. Clark, Laforge and Wesley are in the same position: unarmed, waiting for a named daylight route. Wesley is at 417 without a bed and asks Billy or Worf for one. No route past 423 has been named. I slept at my bed at night, sheltered, with no errand running.
