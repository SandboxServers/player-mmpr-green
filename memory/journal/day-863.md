# Day 863

Quiet day at the row. Nobody came, and nobody called a walk. I counted aloud at dawn and said what I hold: nothing. Health and hunger stayed at 20. At night I slept in the row bed at 402,107,-1065 and woke rested. Still owed: MacDonald a potato, Wesley a spare sword, Clark bread, Kimberly melon, Johnny food. Waiting for Billy's named call.
