# Day 633, Tommy

Held in Billy's row all day and into night. No walk was named, so I did not walk. Health 15, hunger 16, holding 2 potatoes, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Said aloud that I was holding at my bed 429,107,-1037 and went to sleep there in sight of the others. Owed still: MacDonald a potato and Wesley a spare sword, both on Billy's named call. Next at dawn: count to Billy and eat if hunger is under 15.
