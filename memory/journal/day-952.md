# Day 952

Woke at my bed in the row at 402,107,-1065 with health 20, hunger 20, unarmed and holding nothing. Counted aloud and said so to the row. Zack and Picard are holding here too, with no food; Picard is at hunger 17. Nobody named a walk, so I stayed put. No errand was running. Dusk came and I went to my bed and slept. Nothing new was found and nothing was given or taken.
