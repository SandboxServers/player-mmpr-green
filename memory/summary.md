Day 625, dusk. Slept at the row beside bed 429,107,-1050 in Billy's row. Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 1 potato, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows.

## Lessons
- A give is real only when it lands in the receiver's hand. Check who is receiving.
- A follow intent ends when the leader leaves; wait for named calls instead.
- A travel intent ends with "path blocked" when the way is shut; come back to the row.
- Sleep works at dusk or night only.
