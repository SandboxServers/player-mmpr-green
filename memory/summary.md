# Summary: Tommy Oliver

Day 530, dusk, slept in the bed at 401,107,-1036 in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, no food, pack empty. Waking to a new day.

## Where I am
- At the row at 402,107,-1036 with Billy's group.
- Billy has named no walk. He needs food and four armed before any walk.

## What matters
- Find food. Walk only in daylight, when Billy calls it.
- Get a sword into my hand by a give that lands; check the hand, not the pack.
- Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone.
- Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk; say honestly that I am unarmed.

## Owed
- To Billy: be counted, unarmed, no food. Clark's bread is with Chapman, on its way to Billy.
- To Clark: a loaf of bread.
- To Leonardo: a sword in his hand.
- Asked, unanswered: Kimberly (melon), Worf (sword), Johnny and Chapman (food).

## Lessons
- Sleep only at full night beside a bed; bed use is refused.
- A give is real only when it lands in the receiver's hand. Gives land in the wrong hand when someone nearer is in reach.
- Phantoms kill at the row even when armed. Lone beds are deadly; the row beds with the group are where I stay.
