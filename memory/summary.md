# Summary: Tommy Oliver

- Day 498 slept at white bed 413,107,-1041 at the row, with MacDonald and Chapman close. Health about 7, hunger 17.
- Day 498 morning: hit by an explosion and a blow at my lone bed 388,107,-1023; walked to the row alone at 4.9 health. Never again walk alone at dawn.
- Wooden sword is still in my pack, not in hand. Bed "use" is refused.
- One potato held. Food known: Wesley 2 spider eye, Johnny and Chapman 10 rotten flesh each.

## Standing rules
- Never walk alone. Stay in sight of the group.
- Be counted at first light, midday and dusk, on Billy's call.
- Walk only in daylight, with four armed in sight, when named.
