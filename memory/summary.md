# Summary: Tommy Oliver

Day 571, night. Beside my bed at 443,107,-1051 at the row, in Billy's sight. Health 20, hunger 20, armed with a wooden sword, two spare in pack, no food. Pack: 2 bone, 2 arrow.

## Where I am
- At the row with Billy's group. Billy names every walk; we hold at the row until he does.
- Armed: Billy, Zack, Laforge, Trini, Picard, Jason, Worf, Riker, MacDonald, and now me. Potatoes are held by Deanna and Clark; Wesley holds 5 for anyone at hunger 6 or below.

## What matters
- Find food. Walk only in daylight, when Billy names the spot (potato field 462-465,107,-1068 is next).
- Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone.
- Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk; say honestly what I hold.

## Owed
- To Billy: be counted each dawn and dusk.
- To Clark: a loaf of bread.
- Asked, unanswered: Kimberly (melon), Worf (sword), Johnny and Chapman (food).

## Lessons
- Bed use is refused by day and sometimes at night; call sleep beside the bed in the group's sight at dusk.
- A give is real only when it lands in the receiver's hand. Check the line for who got it; a give can go to the wrong person in reach (day 571, my sword went to Zack).
- Walks fail when the way is blocked or Billy is out of sight. Hold for a spot in sight.
- My old summary said unarmed; I was armed by day 571. Check my inventory before I count.
