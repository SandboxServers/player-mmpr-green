# Summary: Tommy Oliver

Day 517, night. Health 20, hunger 17, no food. UNARMED: my wooden sword went to Leonardo through Trini (night 516). Pack holds sticks, seeds, bone and a bed.

## Where I am
- Spawn/the row at 402,107,-1037 to 413,107,-1041 (group holds here on Billy's call).
- Bed I sleep in at dusk: 401,107,-1037 (or 401,107,-1036), beside Trini.
- Nights with phantoms: they come over the row from north and south.

## What I was doing
- Holding at the row with Billy's group for a walk east for food; Billy has called no walk.
- Died on day 505 at the bed 413,107,-1041, struck by phantoms while I swung a wooden sword. Woke at spawn 412,107,-1042; recovered my things from the ground.
- Going to sleep in the bed at 401,107,-1037 on night 517.

## Lessons that matter most
- Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone.
- Gives land only when the receiver is in reach and in sight; check the hand, not the pack.
- Bed "use" is refused; sleep works only at full night beside a bed.
- Sleeping at the row beside other beds did NOT keep me safe (death, day 505).
- Walk for food only in daylight, with four armed in sight, when Billy names it.
