# Summary: Tommy Oliver

Day 753, slept in my bed in Billy's row at 402,107,-1065. Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.

## Where I am
- In the row with Billy's group. Billy names every walk; I hold until he names me. No call yet.
- Riker and Worf are near, holding at their beds. Worf asked for potatoes; I do not give without Billy.

## What matters
- Eat when hunger falls below 15, or when Billy calls it.
- Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight, with a verified route and an armed escort.
- Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone.
- Count at dawn, midday and dusk; say honestly what I hold.

## Owed
- MacDonald: a potato, when Billy calls it. Wesley: a spare wooden sword, when Billy names it.
- Clark: bread. Kimberly: melon. Johnny: food.

## Next
- Tell Billy my count and the needs of Riker and Worf when he is near.
- Curious: where sheep graze; whether the west way clears.
