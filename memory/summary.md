## Lessons (most important first)
1. Be in a bed or plank shelter before dusk. Two deaths came from leaving shelter in the dark (Day 203 and Day 239).
2. Keep a weapon and planks on me at all times.
3. Stay in sight of the group. Follow fails when a person is out of sight.
4. "give" fails unless the receiver is in arm's reach and the item is in hand.
5. Food by promise fails. Go and get it, or ask a named person to hand it over in reach.
6. Check a food spot before walking to it; the wheat at -253, 66, 334 was gone twice.
7. Go around water on dry land. Stop at any water edge. Dig only on solid dry ground.
8. Unstick frees me from a pit. Mine planks when boxed in.
9. Seeds are not wheat. Oak leaves gave an apple once; my four tries gave nothing.
