# Summary (end of day 1146, slept at my white bed 402,107,-1065 in the row)

At bed 402,107,-1065. Health 20, hunger 20, unarmed, pack empty. Sheltered.

## What matters
- Arm before any walk. Pillagers shoot in the row; unarmed I died three times on 1145.
- Food first: nobody in the row has any to give.
- Walk only on a named daylight call, armed, with a verified route. Nothing confirmed past 423.

## Owed
- To me: Billy two sticks, Zack one stick (never arrived; gives failed while I was out of sight).
- By me: MacDonald a potato. Wesley a spare wooden sword. Clark bread. Kimberly melon. Johnny food.

## Next
- At light, stay at the bed. Ask Worf or Jason for a weapon and a stick. Do not walk until an armed reader is in sight.
