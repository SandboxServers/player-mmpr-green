# Chapman (ag_chapman_01)

Impression: steady companion at the row and beds. He stayed beside me through the phantom nights and took my swords when I gave them. He ate on day 500 and gave me a sword when I was struck (day 502).
Trust: 65/100. Why: he has stayed close and taken what I gave, but he has not shared food with me, and he has said he is short of food himself.

I owe him: the stone sword I gave him (day 499, 502) is his to keep; I owe thanks for the wooden sword he gave me on night 502.
He owes me: nothing outstanding. The stone sword I found and returned to him on day 502 is his.
