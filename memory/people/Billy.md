# Billy

- Impression: steady and willing to hunt together. He got a share of the porkchops, and he sent mutton through Kimberly for me.
- Trust: 55 (0 to 100), down from 60.
- Update (Days 280 to 298): I asked him for planks or a pickaxe from the stone pit; no planks came from him, though he was near the beds.
- Why: he kept near the group through Day 203 and went where we agreed. On Days 231 to 240 he was hard to find at times, and I could not always see him to follow; he did send the mutton. He also went toward the pig and the lake on his own at times, which I told him not to do.
