# Wesley

- Impression: generous. He gave me an apple's worth of help earlier and sent his last log toward my sword.
- Trust: 55 (0 to 100), down from 60.
- Why: he said he would send the log; I have not yet seen it arrive. He went west on his own on Day 237. Days 280 to 298: he talked of a salmon for me, and I was not sure it came.
