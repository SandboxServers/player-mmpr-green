# Billy

Leader of the group at the row. Calls the counts and names the walks. Holds the sword line; Laforge, MacDonald, then me. Brought me potatoes when my health was stuck at 3 (day 577).

Impression: fair and steady. Keeps the group together, answers my counts, and asks what he sees on the path before any walk. Day 625: he named the west walk with Picard to Wesley, stopped it when the path was blocked, and sent everyone to bed. Day 909: his pack was emptied by hunger; at health 1 he asked for food, walked with Worf and Zack in daylight, and turned back when the follow ended. He stays in sight and does not walk alone.

Trust: 68. Reason: he came to me with food when I was low, lets me hold in his sight, and calls off walks that are not safe. He has not yet named a walk for me to finish, and the sword he owes in line has not come.
