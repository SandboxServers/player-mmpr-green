# Billy

Leader of the group at the row. Calls the counts and names the walks. Holds the sword line; Laforge, MacDonald, then me.

Impression: fair and steady. Keeps the group together and calls for daylight walks with four armed. He has not named a walk or a sword for me in many days, but he has not turned me away.

Trust: 60. Reason: he answers my counts and lets me hold in his sight; he has not yet given me the sword he owes in line.
