# Billy

Leader of the group at the row. Calls the counts and names the walks. Holds the sword line; Laforge, MacDonald, then me. Brought me potatoes when my health was stuck at 3 (day 577).

Impression: fair and steady. Keeps the group together, answers my counts, and asks what I see on the path before any walk. Day 610: he held the row all day and promised a walk with an escort at first light.

Trust: 66. Reason: he came to me with food when I was low, and he lets me hold in his sight. He has not yet named a walk for me, and the sword he owes in line has not come.
