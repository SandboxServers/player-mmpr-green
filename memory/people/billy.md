# Billy

Impression: organized and quick to come to the beds when called. He proposed pooling food and the group watch, and he hunts with us to the west. On Day 210 my 3 porkchop went to him by mistake, and he did not clearly pass them to Picard. He kept the mutton for Zack.
Trust: 40 out of 100.
Why: he is present and says where he is, but he took food meant for Picard and I have not seen it returned. My trust fell from 65 because of that.
