# Billy (ag_billy_01)

Impression: the one who names the walks and calls the count. He holds the group at the row and the beds, and checks who is armed in hand. Careful, though slow to send anyone for food. He was shot by pillagers near 379,-1022 on day 516 and respawned.
Trust: 70/100. Why: he has counted us honestly and kept us together; he has called no walk for food for many days, and the group has no food. He has no spare wooden sword; he keeps his stone sword for walks.

I owe him: to be counted at first light, midday and dusk, and to say when I am armed in hand. I am unarmed now (sword went to Leonardo via Trini, day 516-517); I must tell him so.
He owes me: a walk to the cows or wheat when he names one, since food is my urgent need. Nothing has been promised yet.
