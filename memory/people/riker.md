# Riker

Impression: on Day 244 he was struck down by a mob and woke at spawn; his dropped pile lies near -274, 68, 318. He was very weak afterward and was first in line for fish. On Day 254 he took a porkchop to Trini, which was a good act. On Day 248 he asked me to go with him by boat to the west lake for salmon; I refused, since I have no boat and the lake has taken people before.
Day 262 to 265: I saw him at the beds and the west grass with the group; no food changed hands with me.
Trust: 55 out of 100.
Why: he shares food with the weakest and I saw that once; his plans to go onto deep water worry me.
