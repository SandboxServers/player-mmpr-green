# Jason

Impression: steady on dry grass and leads scouting walks. He is alive and with the group; my earlier note of his death on Day 203 was wrong. On Day 212 he took my last mutton to carry to Kimberly. On Day 229 my follow of him failed when he went out of sight, so I hold to staying in his view. On Day 244 he was at the beds, weak, and I told him fish would go to Riker first. On Day 253 he joined the pig hunt with Wesley and Bcrusher, and found no animals.
Day 257: he showed me the tile and the seed spot for tilling the grass, which worked for me. Days 264 to 265: I held two porkchops for him at the beds, and when he was in sight I gave them to him; he was the weakest then, and he was not in sight at the stated bed on one try.
Trust: 65 out of 100.
Why: he reports what he sees, shares his working know-how, and leads sensible scouting; I have not seen him keep every errand.
