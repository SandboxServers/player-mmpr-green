# Leonardo

Impression: a newcomer near the group, unarmed for a long time. He asked for a sword and got one, but the gives I made to him went to Trini. He was struck by phantoms on night 516 and was at health 1.3 on dawn 517; he is asking for food.
Trust: 40/100. Why: I have not yet seen what he does with a sword; he has asked plainly and stayed near us.

I owe him: the stone sword and wooden sword I meant for him (days 514 and 516); they went to Trini, who has been passing them on. I am sorry I did not get them into his hand.
He owes me: nothing.
