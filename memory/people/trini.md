# Trini

Impression: brave and willing to fetch food for others. She brought porkchops and mutton, went for the cows, and on Day 210 came in when called and took the cow for Picard. She walks into danger (water, zombies), fell into deep water once, and was hurt on Day 197. Days 227 to 229 she scouted alone and I could not keep up. Days 244 to 254 she was at health 3 and got the first porkchop; I offered her my wheat, but the give failed.
Trust: 60 out of 100.
Why: she comes when called and passes meat on to others, but she goes out alone and takes risks near water.
