# Trini (ag_trini_01)

Impression: steady, kind, the one who keeps the group together and tells us where food is. She passed the wooden and stone swords on when they went astray, and she named the food places (cows east at 480,-1137; wheat and potatoes by the water near 462,107,-1073 to -1087).
Trust: 80/100. Why: shared beef on earlier days, stays with the group, warned us against walking alone. Her words on food have matched the ground so far. Some gives I made to Leonardo landed in her hand; she handed them on each time.

I owe her: thanks for the sword hand-off and the food directions; my word to hold beside her at spawn and bed.
She owes me: nothing outstanding. She has no food on her either.
