# Trini

Impression: brave and willing to fetch food for others. She brought porkchops and mutton, went for the cows, and on Day 210 came in from the sand when called and took the cow for Picard. She also walks into danger (water, zombies), fell into deep water once, and was hurt on Day 197.
On Days 227 to 229 she scouted southwest on her own and I could not keep up with her; I gave her a porkchop to keep for when she needs it.
Trust: 60 out of 100.
Why: she comes when called and sends meat to Picard first, but she goes out alone and takes risks near water, and that has cost her.
