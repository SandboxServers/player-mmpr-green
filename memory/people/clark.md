# Clark (William)

Gave bread that went to me, and I owe him a loaf (his bread went to Chapman). Trust 70 out of 100: he shared without asking. Owed back: bread or a sword when I have one.

Day 1000: greeted me at dawn from his bed in the row, "William here, holding." He holds for Worf's call.

Day 1008: spoke twice at dawn and near dusk from his bed, unarmed, hunger 17, waiting for a named walk. Steady and honest. Trust 72.

Day 1010: at dawn from his bed, "Good morning. William, unarmed, holding." Hunger 17, health steady. Still waiting for Billy or Worf to name a walk. Trust 72.
