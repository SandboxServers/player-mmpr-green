# Troi (Deanna)

Lives in the row beside MacDonald. Gave MacDonald a potato on day 625 and asks him how he is holding.

Impression: caring and keeps her word. Said she would hand me a potato when I come within arm's reach. I have not yet reached her.

Trust: 60. Reason: she has kept her word to MacDonald. She still owes me a potato, not yet collected.
