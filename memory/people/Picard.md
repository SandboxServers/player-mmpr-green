# Picard

- Impression: hungry at the beds, and he took food from me when I could reach him. In the stone pit he promised to bring planks and then left for the night without giving any (Day 297).
- Trust: 20 (0 to 100), down from 45.
- Why: I asked him for four planks at the pit edge over several days (Days 280 to 298). He said he would come back at dawn with planks, and he did not bring them. I gave him a porkchop on Day 236, but I could not confirm it reached him.
