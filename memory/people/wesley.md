# Wesley

Impression: found an apple by breaking oak leaves (Day 229) and gave it to the group; I said Kimberly should get it first, and he agreed. On Day 253 he joined me on the pig hunt, and on Day 254 I went to give him my wheat, but the give failed. He is recorded as "Wcrusher" or "Bcrusher" in some of my notes; I think it is the same person, but I am not sure.
Day 257 to 265: he was at the seed plots and the sheep search with me and Billy. No food changed hands.
Trust: 60 out of 100.
Why: he shares what he finds and accepts the order of the weakest first; I have not seen how he acts when food is short.
