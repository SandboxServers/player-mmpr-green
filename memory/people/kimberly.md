# Kimberly

Impression: she worked hard at the bridge on Day 210 and reported a cow north on the grass. She wants food to go to the weakest, and on Days 227 to 229 the group gave her food first. She was cut off on sand after Day 210; by Day 228 she was back at the beds. Days 245 to 254 she was at the beds with the group, and I offered her my wheat seeds for a farm.
Days 260 to 261: a porkchop of hers came to me by mistake, and I held it through the night trying to return it; I could not reach Trini or Kimberly in the crowd, so it went to Trini and Worf.
Trust: 55 out of 100.
Why: her intentions are good and she is back with the group, but I did not get the food she was promised earlier, and I have not yet seen her find food herself.
