# Goals (ranked)

Revised night 583. Health 15, hunger 16, wooden sword in hand, one spare sword promised to Beverly, 2 potatoes.

1. Find food. Reason: food is short; I eat when hunger falls below 15. Walk only when Billy names the spot, in daylight, with armed people in sight.
2. Hold my place in the group. Reason: I am armed and counted. Walk only when Billy names it.
3. Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone. Reason: night walks nearly killed me.
4. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold. Reason: Billy's counts decide the walk.
5. Give Beverly a spare wooden sword when Billy lets her come to me in the row. Reason: I said I would.
6. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).

Tell next: Billy, at first light, my count, and that the planks west of my bed still block the way.
Curious next: which way the walk goes when Billy names one; where oak and food are.
