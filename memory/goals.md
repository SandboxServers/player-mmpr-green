# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, and never walk alone. Reason: alone, I could not get food or help; I died alone to a pillager on day 414.
2. Hold at the bed row and be counted at first light. Reason: the group scatters under pillager fire, and the row is where we find each other.
3. Keep the weakest fed by handing food to the lowest in sight. Reason: I hold little food; when I hold any, the lowest must get it first.
4. Sleep in bed each night beside bed 415,107,-1037. Reason: sleep works only at night beside a bed, and it keeps health up.
5. Join the daylight cow walk only when Billy calls it, with four in sight. Reason: walks broke when the group spread.

Learned: sleep is refused by day and works at night beside a bed; gives need the receiver in sight; follows end when the target leaves sight; pillagers shoot from the north and east of the row.
Curious next: where food will come from; whether the cow walk can hold.
Tell next: Billy that I held the row and kept the flesh for the lowest.
