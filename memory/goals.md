2. Find food. Reason: I hold no food and hunger is 20, but Billy and others at the row are hungry (17), and food is the thing that keeps us alive.

5. Keep the weakest fed by handing food to the lowest in sight. Reason: I hold none now, so I watch who is lowest and tell the group.
6. Sleep each night in the row's sight, beside a bed. Reason: sleep works only at night beside a bed, and it keeps health up. Sleep worked on day 443 at 388,107,-1050.
7. Join a daylight cow walk only when called, with four in sight and a sword in hand. Reason: walks broke when the group spread, and Billy's last walk failed.
