# Goals (ranked)

Revised day 619 (night). Health 15, hunger 16, 1 potato, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Asleep in my bed in Billy's row, near 444,107,-1063.

1. Find food. Eat when hunger falls below 15, or call Billy at hunger 10. Troi still owes me a potato when I am in her sight; ask Billy to name it. Do not walk to chase food alone.
2. Hold my place in the group. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight. The west walk to Wesley was blocked and Billy turned back, so the route is not verified yet.
3. Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone. Jason of the five is in the row now; I will watch what he does.
4. Before any give, check who is receiving it. A potato meant for MacDonald went to Troi on day 619. Give only to someone I can see and name clearly.
5. Give my spare wooden sword to Wesley when he is in my sight and close, as Billy asked and when Billy calls it. Picard has a sword already; Zack may have covered Wesley, so ask Billy first.
6. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold.
7. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).

Tell next: Billy, at first light, my count; and that the sword is still mine to give Wesley when he calls.
Curious next: whether Troi will share; where oak and food are; where the sheep graze; what Jason knows.
