# Goals (ranked)

Revised day 621 (night). Health 15, hunger 16, 1 potato, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Slept in my bed in Billy's row, near 443,107,-1064.

1. Find food. Eat when hunger falls below 15, or call Billy at hunger 10. Troi still owes me a potato when I am in her sight.
2. Hold my place in the group. Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight. The west way to Wesley is blocked; Billy will try another way.
3. Never walk or sleep alone. Stay in sight of the row.
4. Before any give, check who is receiving it.
5. Give a spare wooden sword to Wesley when Billy calls it and he is close.
6. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold.
7. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).

Tell next: Billy, at first light, my count.
Curious next: whether Troi will share; where food grows; where sheep graze (Worf asked; nobody has seen them yet).
