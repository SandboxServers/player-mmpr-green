# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, and never walk alone. Reason: alone, I could not get food or help; Billy was cut off alone on day 379.
2. Keep myself and the weakest fed, and watch health as well as hunger. Reason: Kimberly fell to 6 health on day 380 even with food near.
3. Join the daylight cow walk only when Billy calls it, with four in sight. Reason: the walks on days 411 and 412 broke when the group spread.
4. Sleep in bed each night: stand next to a bed at the bed row at dusk, wait on day_night, call sleep at night.
5. Hand the 9 rotten flesh to whoever is lowest in sight, in plain view.

Learned: sleep works only at night beside a bed; gives need the receiver in sight; follows end when the target leaves sight.
Curious next: where food will come from; whether the cow walk can hold; where Billy will call the next walk.
Tell next: Billy that I held the row and kept 9 flesh for the lowest.
