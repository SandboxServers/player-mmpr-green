# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, and never walk alone. Reason: alone, I could not get food or help; I died alone to a pillager on day 414.
2. Hold at the bed row and be counted at first light, midday and dusk. Reason: the group scatters under phantom and pillager attack, and the row is where we find each other.
3. Keep the weakest fed by handing food to the lowest in sight. Reason: I hold no food now, so I watch who is lowest and tell Billy.
4. Sleep each night beside bed 415,107,-1037 in the row's sight. Reason: sleep works only at night beside a bed, and it keeps health up. A first sleep can be partial; retry once.
5. Join the daylight cow walk only when Billy calls it, with four in sight and a sword in hand. Reason: walks broke when the group spread.

Learned: sleep is refused by day and when monsters are near; it works at night beside a bed, and a first try can be partial (3 of 10 asleep) before it fully takes; gives need the receiver in sight; follows end when the target leaves sight.
Curious next: where food will come from; whether the cow walk can hold.
