# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, never walk alone. Reason: alone I died to a pillager on day 414.
2. Find food. Reason: I hold none, hunger 20, and others at the row are hungry.
3. Get a weapon for myself. Reason: I am unarmed; Billy's sword and Zack's will not come into hand, so find out how or find a spear.
4. Hold at the row and be counted at first light, midday and dusk, on Billy's call.
5. Keep the weakest fed by handing food to the lowest in sight, and tell the group.
6. Sleep each night beside a bed in the row's sight.
7. Join a daylight cow walk only when named, with four in sight and a weapon in hand.

Learned: a sword only comes into hand when the holder is at ease in daylight with nobody near the chest (unproven). Gives need the receiver in sight and not too close. Chests at the row have held no swords.
