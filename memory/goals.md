# Goals

1. Eat before anything else. Eat when hunger is under 15, asking the weakest holder by name (Trini first).
2. Stay in the group's sight at the bed row, and sleep in my bed there each night.
3. Join a food or stone hunt only when its leader is in my sight.
4. Repay what I owe as soon as I hold the item: MacDonald (potato), Wesley (sword), Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny (food).
5. Collect Billy's sticks when he is in reach.
6. Keep a wooden sword in hand, by a way that works.

Reason: food and safety keep me alive. Food is scarce, so I give it to those weaker than me.
