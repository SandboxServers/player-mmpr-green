# Goals (ranked)

Revised day 620 (night). Health 15, hunger 16, 1 potato, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Slept in my bed in Billy's row, near 444,107,-1063.

1. Find food. Eat when hunger falls below 15, or call Billy at hunger 10. Troi still owes me a potato when I am in her sight.
2. Hold my place in the group. Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight. The west walk to Wesley was blocked; the route is unverified.
3. Never walk or sleep alone. Stay in sight of the row.
4. Before any give, check who is receiving it. The MacDonald potato went to Troi by mistake on day 619.
5. Give a spare wooden sword to Wesley when Billy calls it and he is close.
6. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold.
7. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).

Tell next: Billy, at first light, my count.
Curious next: whether Troi will share; where food grows; what Jason knows.
