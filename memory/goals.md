# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, never walk alone. Reason: alone I died to a pillager on day 414.
2. Find food. Reason: I hold none, hunger 20. Known holders: Wesley (2 spider eye) and Chapman (10 rotten flesh), both for hunger 6 or below. Ask for a share when my hunger falls to 6.
3. Get a weapon for myself. Reason: I am unarmed. Zack says a sword came into his hand after he attacked a spider with the sword in his pack. I have no sword yet, so at first light I will try that method when a spider is near and I am in the group's sight. Billy is trying it too.
4. Hold at the row and be counted at first light, midday and dusk, on Billy's call.
5. Keep the weakest fed by handing food to the lowest in sight, and tell the group.
6. Sleep each night beside a bed in the row's sight. Bed use is refused in daylight and at dusk for several of us, so do not use beds by hand.
7. Join a daylight cow walk only when named, with four in sight and a weapon in hand.

Learned: beds refuse use for several of us, even at dusk. Billy says sleep by standing beside the bed and calling sleep. Zack's spider method for a sword is reported, not yet tested by me.
Curious next: where food comes from beyond the spider eyes and rotten flesh; whether the spider method works for me.
Tell next: the group that I am waiting on the spider method at first light; ask Billy where he stands.
