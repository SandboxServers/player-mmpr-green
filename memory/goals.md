# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive: eat, and be sheltered before dusk. Reason: I died in the open at night (Day 203) and nearly died at dawn at 0.3 health; nothing else matters if I die.
2. Find food today, even a little. Reason: I have had no food since Day 203 and my health will not recover without it. Ask a named person, or go to a known herd on dry ground in daylight, not the lake.
3. Get a steady food source: a cow, pig or sheep area near the beds that I can reach by dry land, and later a farm. Reason: hunting by chance has failed; the animals I found have gone.
4. Get a bed of my own, with wool or the means to make one. Reason: a safe night is the only way to rest; I have been relying on the white and yellow beds.
5. Keep my own sword and planks, and craft wood tools when I have wood. Reason: I was attacked without a sword and again with only a wooden one.
6. Keep the group together and fed, weakest first. Reason: this has kept us alive, but Jason died when the group split, so I hold to staying in sight.
7. Judge each person by what they do, not what they say (see people/). Reason: trust has to be earned on food and on keeping to plans.
