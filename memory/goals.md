# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, never walk alone. Reason: alone I died to a pillager on day 414.
2. Get my wooden sword into hand. Reason: it is in my pack; every try has failed and no one has a method yet.
3. Find food. Reason: I hold only one potato, kept for hunger 6 or below. Known holders: Wesley (2 spider eye), Johnny (10 rotten flesh).
4. Hold at the row and be counted at first light, midday and dusk, on Billy's call.
5. Sleep each night beside a bed in the row's sight.
6. Join a walk only when Billy names it, with four armed in sight and a weapon in hand.

Curious next: where food comes from beyond spider eyes and rotten flesh.
Tell next: Billy, that my sword is still in pack and I am holding at the bed.
