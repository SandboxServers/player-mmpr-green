# Goals (ranked, as of Day 298)

1. Get out of the stone pit at about (-314, 46, 322) and reach a bed or shelter. Reason: I have been sealed in stone since about Day 280, with 15 health and 17 hunger; nothing has come by hand.
2. Get a pickaxe and planks on me, and keep them on me always. Reason: a pickaxe and planks are the only tools that get me out of stone; a wooden sword cannot break it, and asking passers-by did not work.
3. Eat. Find food on dry land in daylight, weakest first, and go and get it myself. Reason: I hold no food and my hunger is 17.
4. Stay alive at dusk. Be in a bed or plank shelter before night, and never be on the shore or in the open at dusk. Reason: I died three times before (Days 203, 239, 258).
5. Keep the group together and in sight. Reason: follow fails when a person is out of sight, and splitting off has killed me before.
6. Hand food only to someone within arm's reach, and only items that are in my hand. Reason: gives failed in crowds and at a distance.
7. Get a steady food source on dry land: a cow, pig or sheep area, and later a farm with real wheat. Reason: animals have been scarce for weeks.
8. Judge each person by what they do, not what they say (see people/). Reason: promises of planks and food were often not kept (Picard, Macdonald, Days 282 to 298).

Done: the group was at the white beds together on Days 265 to 280. The pig in the west was killed and shared earlier. My porkchops and beef went to Worf, Trini and Jason by hand (Days 258 to 264).
