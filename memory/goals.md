# Goals (ranked)

Revised day 764 (night). Health 15, hunger 16. Hold 3 potatoes, 1 wooden sword, 2 bone, 2 arrows.

1. Keep food honest: eat when hunger falls under 15, or when Billy calls it. (Reason: health is my margin.)
2. Hold my place in the row. Walk only on Billy's named call, in daylight, with an armed escort and a verified route.
3. Never walk or sleep alone. Stay in sight of the row; keep Riker and Worf in view.
4. No give without Billy. Riker and Worf ask for potatoes; refused until Billy names it.
5. Watch Jason and Trini: trust by what they do on a named walk.
6. Give a spare wooden sword to Wesley only when Billy names it.
7. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold.
8. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny (food). MacDonald: potato, when Billy calls it.

Tell next: Billy, when he is near, my count, and Riker's and Worf's need for food.
Curious next: where sheep graze; whether the west way clears.
