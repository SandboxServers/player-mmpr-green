# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, and never walk alone. Reason: alone, I could not get food or help; Billy was cut off alone on day 379.
2. Keep myself and the weakest fed, and watch health as well as hunger. Reason: Kimberly fell to 6 health on day 380 even with food near.
3. Join the daylight cow walk only when Billy calls it, with the group in reach.
4. Sleep in bed each night: stand next to a bed at the bed row at dusk, wait on day_night, call sleep at night.
5. Help Billy come back into the group's sight; he was hurt at health 4 on night 380. Do not go out alone to find him.
6. Recover Jason's pile at 411,-1055 with the group in daylight.
7. Judge the friends by deeds. So far deeds match words.

Learned: sleep works only at night beside a bed; gives need the receiver in sight.
Curious next: where food will come from; where the bows come from; whether Billy is healed.
