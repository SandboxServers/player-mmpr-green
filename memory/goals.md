# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, never walk alone. Reason: alone I died to a pillager on day 414.
2. Find food. Reason: I hold none. Known holders: Wesley (2 spider eye) and Chapman (10 rotten flesh), both for hunger 6 or below. Lead: Chapman says the cow field is at 339,104,-979, named by Billy; walk there only when Billy names it. Ask for a share when my hunger falls to 6.
3. Get a weapon for myself. Reason: I am unarmed. Zack's method (attack a spider with the sword in pack) is still untested by me; Billy and Picard are trying it.
4. Hold at the row and be counted at first light, midday and dusk, on Billy's call.
5. Sleep each night beside a bed in the row's sight.
6. Join a walk only when Billy names it, with four in sight and a weapon in hand.

Learned: Billy says sleep by standing beside the bed and calling sleep; partial sleep needs a second call. Spiders have not come into reach since day 455. Cow field reported at 339,104,-979.
Curious next: where food comes from beyond spider eyes and rotten flesh; whether the spider method works.
Tell next: Billy, when a spider comes near, that I am ready to try it.
