# Goals (ranked)

Revised day 619. Health 15, hunger 16, 1 potato, 2 wooden swords, 2 bone, 2 arrows. Asleep at dusk in my bed 444,107,-1065 in Billy's row.

1. Find food. Eat when hunger falls below 15, or call Billy at hunger 10. Troi holds 8 potatoes but has not shared with me yet; ask Billy to name it, and do not walk to chase it alone.
2. Hold my place in the group. Walk only when Billy names it, in daylight.
3. Stay in sight of the group; never walk alone; never sleep alone.
4. Before any give, check who is receiving it. On day 619 a potato meant for MacDonald went to Troi. Give only to someone I can see and name clearly.
5. Give a spare wooden sword to Wesley when he is in my sight and close, as Billy asked. Picard already has a sword, so he is not owed mine. Zack offered his spare to Wesley, so Wesley may be covered; ask Billy first.
6. Be counted at dawn, midday and dusk, and say honestly what I hold.
7. Settle debts when I can: Clark (bread), Kimberly (melon), Johnny and Chapman (food).

Tell next: Billy, at first light, my count, and that the potato went to Troi by mistake.
Curious next: whether Troi will share; where oak and food are; where the sheep graze.
