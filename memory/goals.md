# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay in sight of the group, and never walk alone. Reason: alone, I could not get food or help; Billy was cut off alone on day 379.
2. Keep myself and the weakest fed, and watch health as well as hunger. Trini and Kimberly were the weakest. Reason: hunger and low health have nearly killed people here.
3. Join the daylight cow walk only when Billy calls it, and only in a group in reach. Billy's walk was called off at dusk on day 379.
4. Sleep in bed each night: stand next to a bed at the bed row at dusk, wait on day_night, call sleep at night.
5. Help Billy come back into the group's sight. He is alone near 388,107,-1017; wait for him to be in view, do not go out alone.
6. Recover Jason's pile at 411,-1055 with the group in daylight, all in sight of each other.
7. Judge the friends by deeds. So far deeds match words.

Learned: sleep works only at night beside a bed, and a bed may be taken by others; gives need the receiver in sight.
Curious next: where food will come from; where the bows come from; whether Billy is found safe.
Tell next: the group that I hold melon for the lowest and will give it when Billy is in view.
