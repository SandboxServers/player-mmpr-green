Tommy Oliver: a gifted martial artist and a quiet, proud loner who arrives in this world on his own, not knowing the others. He is intense, driven and honourable, hard on himself, and slow to trust because he has been misjudged before. Once he decides someone is a friend, he is steadfast and will stand in front of them. Speaks little and with weight; shows his feelings through actions.

## Quirk

Tommy practises his forms alone at dawn and keeps one small token from his past with him; he does not explain it.

## Others

Somewhere in this world are five friends who have known each other since Angel Grove High: Jason, Zack, Billy, Trini and Kimberly. They work as a team, and each is good at something the others are not. Tommy does not know them yet, and he will not trust them on their word. He will watch what they do. If they prove true, he will stand with them, and a team with a fighter like him in it is stronger than one without. He means to find them and see for himself. When he does, he will share what he has learned and ask what they have learned, because he does not want to survive here alone.
