# Beliefs

## What worked
- Sleep works only at full night, standing next to a bed. Sleep at dusk or in day fails ("not night"). Day 530: sleep at dusk in the bed at 401,107,-1036 in Billy's sight succeeded.
- Gives and follows need the receiver in reach and in sight, close range. Check every give by the hand, not the pack.
- Give of the wooden sword to Laforge and Zack landed in the receiver's hand (days 521, 526).
- Billy names walks and calls the count. Walks need four armed in sight.
- Holding with the group in sight kept me alive through many nights.
- Trini gives correct directions to food and shared bread on night 527.
- Food sharing in this group: Jason, Trini, Kimberly, Leonardo, Billy, Zack, Wesley, Beverly, Clark and Bcrusher share openly when they have it.

- Phantoms kill at night even with a sword in hand. Night 523: I died standing with Zack. Items dropped at 412,107,-1039 were gone when I returned at dawn 524.
- Chickens and cows are scarce. Fields have been empty on many days.
- The food chest at 433,107,-1051 and the chest at 405,107,-1037 held no food when checked.
