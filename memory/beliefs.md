# Beliefs

- A wooden sword goes into my hand with a "use" and no target. Using it on a block does not work ("that block cannot be used that way").
- Sleeping at the bed row in the group's sight skips the night.
- Beds at the row refuse some people at night (Balboa was refused on 1213).
- Trini leads the group's legs. Legs that go out without naming me leave me at the row.
- Water and cows have not been found yet near the row; Trini plans an east search.
- Lone walking is dangerous; stay in sight.
