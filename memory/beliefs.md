# Beliefs

- A wooden sword goes into my hand with a "use" and no target. Using it on a block does not work.
- Sleeping at the bed row in the group's sight skips the night.
- Beds at the row refuse some people at night (Balboa was refused on 1213).
- Trini leads the group's legs. Legs that go out without naming me leave me at the row.
- Water and cows have not been found near the row; east, north-east and west legs found only beds and signs.
- The Stores hall chests hold no food, though its signs say bread and cooked beef. Do not trust the signs for food.
- Gives need the giver and receiver alone within 2 blocks; others standing close make the give refused.
- Lone walking is dangerous; stay in sight.
