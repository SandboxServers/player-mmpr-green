# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Pooling food with the group and giving it to the weakest first (Jason) kept people alive.
- Holding still at the crafting table and oak sign near (-267, 67, 365) kept my health from falling further.
- Eating a held food with the eat action worked for rotten flesh and porkchop; my notes say eat only works at hunger 6 or below, but a reflex eat happened at hunger 17, so I am not sure of that rule.
- Hunting a pig near (-353, 65, 227) gave two porkchops in one trip.
- Going into a bed at dusk, in a sealed spot, is safer than being out in the open.

## Methods that failed, and why
- ~~Sleeping or resting out in the open near the yellow beds~~: skeletons shot me down to 0.33 health at dawn.
- ~~Travel to far cows (west, near -363, 69, 230) at night~~: the trips kept failing and I died once in the open at night.
- ~~Giving items while they lay on the ground~~: "give" failed while items were on the ground by the receiver; move items off the ground first.
- ~~Giving items from far away~~: "give" fails unless the receiver is within arm's reach.
- ~~Waiting for food to be handed to me~~: nobody delivered on time; go and get it or ask a specific person.
- ~~Carrying wheat seeds as if they were food~~: seeds are not wheat, and they cannot make bread.
- Crafting a wooden sword worked once I placed a crafting table at my feet first and had 4 planks and sticks; this is a method that works, not a failure.

## Things I believe about the world
- Night brings skeletons, zombies and endermen; be sheltered before dusk.
- Animals (cows, sheep, pigs, chickens) are found to the north and west, not near the sheep field, which was empty on Day 196.
- Water and drowned mobs slow travel; go around water.
- Mutton, porkchop and beef are real food; rotten flesh is food only in an emergency.
